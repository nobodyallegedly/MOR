# mor-anchoring

**Experimental**: an instrument for testing the core, never for real money. The anchoring cMIP (`../cmip-anchoring-draft-7.md`; drafts 2 to 6 kept, superseded), the Envelopes' anchoring task; its first clock is the Bitcoin clock Module (`../../modules/module-bitcoin-clock-draft-2.md`, in crate `mor-onchain`). The core's own interface (`mor_core::envelopes::anchoring`) is unchanged.

In plain words: a service sells anchors the way anyone sells anything on MOR now. It signs a **publication** (it sells alone, and receives its own money) whose terms are this cMIP's tiers and deadlines, and **one price tag per tier** naming it (draft 7, F409). A payer pays its tier's tag and gets a ticket; the service anchors the payer's fingerprint in a batch and signs a batch notice; if it leaves the fingerprint out or misses the deadline, the price is owed back. The core says whether the publication and the tag count and whether a payment is a purchase; this crate says whether the anchor was delivered (draft 6, round 8: the standing offer of drafts 3 to 5 is gone from the core; draft 7: one tag per tier).

- `src/tree.rs`: the batch tree: leaves `H_leaf(act || blind)`, nodes and the root under three distinct tags, the count bound into the root, one canonical proof per leaf (F200 applied to the batch).
- `src/service.rs`: the pooled anchoring service: its terms (urgency tiers, each priced, or priced by a scheme on the fee rate, F228), sold under an Agreements publication, a lone seller's, the service its own split service (`Terms::publication`), priced by one price tag per tier naming it (`Terms::price_tags`; draft 7, F409), read together as its offer (`Offer::from_publication` for one tag, `Offer::from_publication_tags` matching each tag to its tier, `Offer::tier_of`; `Offer::read`, from a standing offer, is removed: nothing reads it any more); the ticket for each paid hash (its batch, deadline and quote), the batch notice of each batch (its leaves and transaction; type 3, `Publication`, alias `BatchNotice`), both signed under the publication; the judgment: delivered, provably omitted, late, a default, or not paid, each default a refund owed under the offer's terms; where a refund stands, given what Money shows repaid; and the payer's client check of a ticket before paying.
- `src/custody.rs`: the pool as visible custody (draft 4, items 21 to 24): each batch's money followed from the payments buying its tickets, through what Money shows paid back, to its transaction (the coins it spends and the change it pays back, each shown by the service's receipt of a payment to itself), its fee, and what the service kept, a loss shown as one; the clock Module reads the transaction (`PoolChain`).
- `tests/batch.rs`: the tree: domain separation, one proof per leaf and its twin refused, a batch root never a payment commitment, an inner node never a leaf.

The service is tested on the Bitcoin clock in `../../modules/onchain/tests/service.rs` (Lightning payments per hash, real BOLT 11 invoices signed by a test node key), against the core's Agreements view in `../../modules/onchain/tests/agreement.rs` (the publication counts, its tag counts under it, the payment of the tag is a purchase under the publication, a default refunded and shown repaid), the custody in `../../modules/onchain/tests/custody.rs`, the batch anchors in `../../modules/onchain/tests/anchor.rs`, the theft rule with a batch-anchored lock change in `../../modules/onchain/tests/money.rs`, and end to end on regtest in `../../harness/tests/anchoring.rs`:

```
MOR_BTCD=/path/to/btcd-dir cargo test -p mor-harness --test anchoring -- --nocapture
```

With the Lightning regtest network of `../../modules/lightning/regtest/up.sh` running, add `MOR_LN_REGTEST=RUN_DIR` and every payment, refunds included, moves over real lnd nodes.
