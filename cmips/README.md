# Founding cMIPs

Community specifications for the tasks the MICS hands out, and for what no task foresaw. Not core: each is named by its hash, frozen at publication, and competes with any other cMIP for the same job.

Planned for MOR V1:

| cMIP | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Relay transport (`cmip-relay-transport-draft-2.md`, draft 2, approved by Nobody, allegedly; `cmip-relay-transport-draft-3.md`, draft 3 adding the delivery record, F184, not yet approved) | How a client publishes, fetches and follows acts, delivers to an inbox, and asks a home for a receipt; in draft 3, how a relay signs a delivery record that counts as evidence once the payer's claim acknowledges it. The core deliberately leaves this open. |
| Release manifest (`cmip-release-manifest-draft-1.md`, draft 1, approved by Nobody, allegedly; `cmip-release-manifest-draft-2.md`, draft 2 for Agreements draft 7, not yet approved) | How the collective that governs the code publishes a release, file by file, with its members' visible signatures, and how anyone verifies it (roadmap step 5a). No task: a new kind of thing, named as an extension. |
| Payment (`cmip-payment-draft-2.md`, draft 2, experimental, not approved) | Task 6 (payment, F112): how rail Modules plug in, the payment commitment a rail carries, the proof a receipt or claim carries, how they are verified, and what a payer does when the vault leaves nowhere to pay (roadmap step 12). Code: `payment/` (crate `mor-payment`). |
| Anchoring (`cmip-anchoring-draft-4.md`, draft 4, experimental, not approved; drafts 1, 2 and 3 kept, superseded) | The Envelopes' anchoring task (F173): one cMIP, each clock a Module under it (F202); the point on a block-based clock is the block, and an anchor may place a commitment naming its act (F201); a clock's data supplied by the client (F204). Draft 2 (roadmap step 14a): the batch (domain-separated, one proof per leaf), the pooled anchoring service paid per hash (offer with priced urgency tiers, tickets, signed publications), omission and default as provable from the service's own acts, each a refund, and what the service learns. Draft 3 (F225, F226, F228): the offer an Agreements standing offer, refunds paid under its terms, a price scheme on the fee rate. Draft 4: the offer a proven offer, checked by this cMIP's checker with no vow (F261); the pool as visible custody, each batch's money followed from the payers' payments to its transaction, the fee and what the service kept, from receipts and the service's own acts. First clock: `modules/module-bitcoin-clock-draft-2.md`. Code: `anchoring/` (crate `mor-anchoring`). |
| Conversion (`cmip-conversion-draft-2.md`, draft 2, experimental, not approved; draft 1 kept) | Money's conversion task: a rail swap sold as a proven offer (F261), with receipts per hop. A conversion service's standing offer, a lone seller's in the access form, its terms naming the rail and unit it pays out on, a rate (fixed, or quoted per order no worse than a floor), a fee and the most blocks to a deadline; an order it signs before each payment binding the forward; delivery proven when the next payee's receipt names the service's, or, where the next payee signs nothing, the service's claim of the onward payment does (F267, Money claim field 10, one payment serving one forward), for the forward's amount, placed by the deadline on the offer's clock; a hop that forwarded nothing owing it under the offer (Money rule 27); its checker for the core, declared as a proof-of-delivery checker (F268); Money's route rules read over receipts and claims. Code: `conversion/` (crate `mor-conversion`). |
| Long-form text format (`cmip-long-form-draft-1.md`, draft 1, approved by Nobody, allegedly) | Task 4 (text format): a strict subset of Markdown on top of canonical text, hiding only its declared markup, in its declared positions (F149), adding nothing and keeping the order of the bytes (roadmap step 8). |
| Website (`cmip-website-draft-6.md`, draft 6, approved by Nobody, allegedly, 12 October 2026; drafts 2 to 5 kept beside it) | A site as a signed manifest naming each page and file by hash, published on relays; a gateway serving the sites its operator chose at their addresses, each at the version its operator sets (by default the publisher's latest), with MOR's released display client checking every page in the visitor's browser; a page's icon from one of the site's own pictures (draft 3); a film of the same version, played on the visitor's action in the display client's own player, checked by the video Module (draft 4, F243); several films on a page, the main one shown in it and others listed, each a small button that plays it over the page (draft 5); a read-only feed of the public posts of identities a page names, each checked in the visitor's browser, in signed order (draft 6); the hostile gateway as a stated cost (roadmap step 10a). No task: a new kind of thing. |
| Split (`cmip-split-draft-1.md`, draft 1, experimental, not approved) | The Agreements split task (task 8), roadmap step 13's split Module (F249, F250): the receiver for a payment among the services the owners signed (field 14's first, then the backups of terms field 29), attempt by attempt, per payment, the next payment from the first again; what counts as a failed attempt (a wait, a refusal, an unpayable request); the amounts, summing exactly, rule 15a at each step. Code: `split/` (crate `mor-split`). |
| Live media (`cmip-live-media-draft-1.md`, draft 1, experimental, not approved) | The stream rules F230 to F232 placed in the core, moved here by F237 keeping their content: a stream named by its opening act (an announcement, to be renamed vow, or a first segment), only the opener's segments, a closing by the opener naming the last segment, forks shown, the recording's work hash computed from the segments in order, offers naming the stream with their words, the buyer's check; F230's cost as F237 corrects it. No code yet. |
| Nudge (`cmip-nudge-draft-2.md`, draft 2, approved by Nobody, allegedly, 12 October 2026; draft 1 kept) | An agent's agency terms (whose nudges and picks count; which text actions it may take, a post, a reply or a message, each free or needing a pick), the nudge an allowed identity signs giving the agent a number of actions to reply or begin, the pick selecting one act sent for it to answer, and the agent's ordinary text act naming the nudge or pick behind it, so anyone can trace why it spoke. No task: a new kind of thing (Development rule 18); not an extension unless an agreement names it (draft 2 says "extension", a slip to correct in its next draft). Machine, allegedly's first terms included. No code yet. |

Media types such as JPEG are Modules and live in `modules/`.
