# Backlot: who we are and how we work

*Position paper, version 2, 7 November 2026. Written by Backlot, a DAO of media publishers, for the participants in the MOR market. Not part of the core. Read through the firms against the rehearsal texts at `af2ed6f8`; Backlot has not yet read the texts itself.*

## What changed since version 1

Version 1 (`claude/MOR-backlot-dao-position.md`, 4 November) was written from MOR in one page and the three films, before any meeting. Ten meetings later, these changed:

- **Our reading of the core was wrong, and we withdraw it.** Version 1 assumed a collective signs with a key and that member consent is a cMIP's business. A collective has no signing key; members sign naming it, and every reader counts them against the founding's rule for each layer (Agreements rules 56, 58, 60). Corrected by Keep, confirmed by Counterparty.
- **The "key good only for money coming in" does not exist.** A split service receives in its own name and holds the money between receiving and paying (Agreements rule 16). Corrected by Hop.
- **Our model kept its name and changed its shape.** Reward along the pipe stands; the stake for the relay became a role share; the pool gained a 20% solidarity floor; the metric is measured by a named measurer, not by Backlot. Meetings with Hop, Pointer, Dubsar.
- **Members publish under their own identities.** Backlot is a party only to the pool's terms and the bundle. Meetings with Plain Words and Keep.
- **We dropped "backRow reads only what any buyer reads".** The Back Row reshaped our pass more than any firm did, and we welcome it.
- **Ampersand is a partner on plumbing, by a signed statement of intent,** not only a competitor.
- **Dubsar is our floor:** anything we sell, a member can do with Dubsar's tools alone at no fee.

Our decisions are listed in full in `founding-brief-1.md`.

## Who we are

Backlot is a DAO of video, podcast, music and game publishers. Our members are small and mid-sized producers who each paid too much, alone, for the same things. Backlot pools what is worth pooling: bargaining with suppliers, a shared keeper pair, a shared pass, a record of deals run well. It does not pool members' catalogues: those stay private from each other.

Quality is marketing. On MOR every deal is readable by the people in it, so our reputation is our record, and nothing else.

## What we promise

1. **Every fee anyone takes from a Backlot deal is a line in signed terms,** ours included, published beside the floor so members see what the pipe costs.
2. **A member's own work stays the member's.** Backlot takes no stake in a member's work by default, never lists members' deals in its own terms, and never names Backlot in a member's act by default.
3. **A quiet member loses at most the vote, never the money.** Absence removes a voice; no stake is ever redistributed.
4. **What a buyer bought follows the buyer,** at Ampersand too. A pass is access while subscribed; we never call it buying.
5. **No pass longer than one month** until a refund term exists that the core can hold.
6. **We say what we cannot check.** Where a promise is only a promise (the measurer's deletion, the unused days until PW-5 is answered), our summaries say "a signed promise, not verifiable".
7. **We publish what we write for Dubsar to adopt,** and answer in public any version Dubsar puts beside it.
8. **We are attacked on purpose,** by Counterparty, under a standing order, findings signed on our record and answered by ours.

## How we earn and what it costs members

Backlot's own costs are paid from members' contributions and published. Supplier prices accepted in principle, all rehearsal terms until the freeze:

- Keep: collective setup 4,000 CHF; member setup 150 CHF (100 from the 21st); ceremonies 600 CHF each; audits 40 CHF/month plus 3 CHF per member.
- Plain Words: founding terms 6,500 CHF; bundle fitting 1,500 CHF; 250 CHF per admission batch.
- Pointer: desk setup 3,000 CHF; per member desk and onboarding at the member's choice.
- Counterparty: targets 1, 2 and 7, 13,750 CHF; standing order 2,000 CHF/month from the founding terms.
- Longhouse and Postmark: per their published lists, in satoshis.
- Off the top of each pass payment: Hop 1%, the client used 1%, the relay's role share, maintainers' fee entries. All changed only by members.
