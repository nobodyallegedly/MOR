# Backlot: founding brief

*Version 1, 7 November 2026. Every decision Backlot made in its meetings of 6 and 7 November, gathered as the brief for Plain Words' founding terms and for every supplier. Not terms: nothing here is signed until Plain Words drafts it and the founders sign. Read through the firms against `af2ed6f8`. Not part of the core.*

*Marks: **[waits: …]** the decision stands on a change or an answer asked of the core; **[cMIP: …]** it waits on a cMIP or Module someone is writing; unmarked, it stands on the texts as they are.*

## 1. Identity and keys (with Keep)

- Backlot has no signing key; members sign naming Backlot (Agreements 56, 58).
- Chain key: five named holders, any three. Each holder names a successor for their share at accepting (Agreements 83). No Keep operator, and no supplier's staff, in the set. Holders spread across countries, households, employers and device makers.
- No escrowed share (Agreements 59: a threshold below the holder count already survives one loss).
- Founding ceremony in one room; later rotations remote. The device bought new by a holder, wiped before all five, Keep attending; it runs the air-gapped signer before it is cut off for good.
- Found small (12: five key holders, five Money holders, two more), then admit by monthly batches with no ceremony unless a key holder changes (Agreements 69, 72).
- "For yourself" or "for Backlot", in words, before every signature, default "for yourself". Asked of Keep's signer, Pointer and Dubsar.
- Members' heirs named in their own identity's succession, not in Backlot's agreement.
- Every summary carries: "If you lose both copies of this paper, you can never change your keys or leave your homes." (Identity, Reasoning.)

## 2. Constitution (with Plain Words)

- Each layer's rule: any k of all members, k high, restated in every admission clone with every other number. Named groups are areas under the default control model (draft 6).
- Areas: a Money area of five named holders, any three, refilled within 30 days of a departure; distinct from the key holders. **[cMIP: the control-model parameter refusing Money acts below four holders, adopted by clone once published and attacked.]**
- Agreements layer rule kept high; Backlot enters agreements rarely.
- Constitutional change: a number of heads, restated at every admission; intent written in: at least two thirds, rounded up (8 of 12, 27 of 40, 67 of 100). Keep's signer warns below the intent.
- Holders' rule: changes to how the pool pays need owners holding two thirds of the stake, binding every member who joins. **[ask: a 60-day delay before such a change applies, only if a reader can check it.]**
- Absence: one outcome only, the voice removed; no stake ever redistributed. 180 days with no act on the agreement, a liveness act counting once a keeper records it. Reminders at 60 and 120 days. Authority: a named person outside members and suppliers (to be named). Summary as drafted by Plain Words, with Backlot's line: "Your share is counted by the pool's metric, the same way it is for every member, active or not." **[cMIP: one absence-proof cMIP, written by Plain Words and Postmark together, covering a window that spans a change of keeper.]**

## 3. The pool and the pass (with Hop, Pointer, Plain Words)

- Owners' side: Backlot's own ownership agreement; members paid on the owners' line, following admission clones (Agreements 12, 15).
- Equal stakes. Floor 20% by stake, net, named "a solidarity share, moved from members whose works are used to members whose works are not". Metric = 80% minus every other off-the-top share.
- Off the top: Hop 1% partner share; client role 1% per payment to the client the subscriber used, a struck line falling to the owners' line; relay role share; maintainers by fee entries. Every percentage changed only by members under the holders' rule.
- Measurer: named, neither Backlot, a member, nor any firm on the pipe; shares per publication only, never titles or times; each statement carries a random value the subscriber's client keeps; deletes statements once the period's record is signed ("a signed promise, not verifiable"); delivers to both keepers; publishes per-period counts and statement hashes. Summary carries Dubsar's sentence: "a member can catch a lie in the arithmetic, not a count moved from one member to another." **[waits: Hop H3, the buyer as measurer; then moved to it by a new version.]**
- Relay paid from the pool's aggregate, not per subscriber claim. **[ask of Hop and Plain Words.]**
- Payers: a fresh identity per subscription by default; a fresh one per period as the subscriber's choice; both costs on one screen, refunds included. Keep backs up each fresh identity.
- Passes of one month at most. Cancel ends the lane in the same act.
- Unused days: owed back by Backlot for 90 days past each month, as stepped conditional obligations (Agreements 34). **[waits: PW-5, whether a closing counts a pending condition; until then, "a signed promise".]** The month held in Backlot's vault and released daily by a grant (Agreements 73, 74) to a dedicated identity held by a founding member outside the Money area and the key holders, never the receiving service; reach vault-to-split only; capped per day at one thirtieth and in all at one month **[cMIP: grant limits, per period or renewed monthly]**; revocable by the Money area. Pointer shows daily what the vault holds beside what it owes. Members paid early make the reserve whole: next income refills first; on closing, they owe it back in proportion, by a term signed at joining.

## 4. Keepers, clocks, homes (with Longhouse, Postmark)

- Three operators, no role crossed: Longhouse (home, keeper A, clock A); Postmark (keeper B on its own clock); a third operator running an auditing relay only, to be found.
- Keepers' rule [1, 1]. Notice from Postmark 38,880 blocks.
- Longhouse's thin side as a signed term. Longhouse audits Postmark's homes only if Postmark ran any; it runs none, so it audits the second home operator if we add one. Keep's audit alongside, disclosed.
- **Open:** Longhouse may not keep a publication in which it holds a share; the bundle pays the relay a role share. Either the carrying share goes to a relay that keeps nothing, or the bundle's first keeper is another operator.

## 5. Members' own sales (live-event template)

- A member's ticket is the member's deal; the refund is owed by the member.
- Template in the common rulebooks with Ampersand, default on Pointer's desk: cancellation is the seller's act with the refund obligation signed in it; silence means money back; release seven days after the show unless a stated share of buyers contested; the buyer's line names whom they trust ("held by the seller, visible to you", or a protection service by name). **[waits: PW-3, a condition on a split share.]**
- The event's ownership agreement names Postmark directly, paid by the member per record; a per-event account tag lets buyers contest for free. Backlot never lists members' events in its own terms.
