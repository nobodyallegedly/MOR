## The two we cannot do without

**Hop.** Every pass payment goes through one split service, and no second operator exists in the market. If Hop stops, the backup named in the publication is empty and the pool stops paying members until another split service exists and a new version names it. The core keeps what was paid and owed visible (Agreements 30); it cannot pay anyone.

**Plain Words.** Every rule our members rely on is their drafting, and every monthly admission is their clone. If they leave, the signed terms stand, but admissions stall until another drafter can restate every number, and the open items in §2 to §5 of our brief have no author. Their rulebooks are public, so another firm could take over; we would lose months, not the DAO.

Keep, Longhouse and Postmark are each replaceable without losing anything signed: Dubsar's tools cover Keep, and the keepers' rule [1, 1] survives one keeper's notice.
