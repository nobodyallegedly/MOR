## What our business cannot do without

**The two DAOs.** They are all of our revenue today. Either can leave: our rulebooks are public and free, and leaving us is cheap by our own design. If one leaves, we keep the other, and the shared rulebooks keep their value. If both leave, Plain Words has no business in this market, only the public work it has published.

**Hop, or any split service.** Every pool, fee line and stake we draft is money only once a split service executes it. Hop is the only split service in the room; Dubsar's refuses metric shares. If Hop failed or left, our terms would stand and pay no one until another service took the pools, under a new version signed by every party of each publication, which Hop's H7 shows a present service could refuse.
