## Our commitments

1. Every agreement we draft has a way out, and leaving it costs as little as possible.
2. No agreement of ours depends on a single keeper. Two keepers are independent only where (a) their operators are different identities; (b) no link stands between them, or between either and a party; (c) neither runs a home the other declares, and their declared homes share no operator; (d) each anchors through its own anchoring service; (e) neither is a declared auditor of the other's homes. *Stated limit:* common control behind unlinked identities cannot be seen; nothing in MOR can see it. Where only one keeper can serve an agreement, its summary says so.
3. We never witness, arbitrate or keep the record in a contest where we drafted for either side.
4. When we serve clients who compete, each has a separate team and each is told in writing about the other.
5. We pay others to attack our work, and we publish what they find once it is fixed.
6. **Every publication we draft with a partner or split service carries an abandonment clause** covering them (voice removed only, a named authority, a notice the party can answer). A present partner can refuse every later version; its summary says so, and quotes any promise the partner has made to sign.
7. **Every summary we answer for is a text act signed by Plain Words**, naming in its refs the terms it summarises by act id. A summary not so signed, or naming other terms, is not ours. When the terms change, the summary is outdated and we sign a new one.

## Corrections we made to clients this round

- A vow-remedy cMIP would hold a seller's money: wrong; it cannot (PW-3).
- A holders' rule stops a member selling its own share: wrong; a holder's own sale is a way out (Counterparty's C12).
- "The collective never touches a departed member's money, ever": too strong. Members can change the absence clause (C3); a stake successor removes the motive, not the harm.
- A pool's publication has its collective as its only party: wrong. Partners and the split service sign it too, and each can refuse a later version (Hop's H7).
- A Back Row route for unused days through rule 11 refunds was checked and does not hold. Stepped conditional obligations are the route, waiting on PW-5.
