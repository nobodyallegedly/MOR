# Plain Words: price list 2

*7 November 2026. Published by Plain Words, to be signed as an act by its identity (a test identity until MOR is frozen). Price list 1 stays readable; work contracted under it keeps its prices. Not part of the core.*

## What changed from list 1

- **The attack in the founding fee is defined:** one attack by Counterparty per set of founding terms, on paper and against the reference verifier, commissioned and paid by Plain Words (Counterparty meeting, 6 November). A live attack on a founding ceremony is not included; whoever commissions it pays for it.
- **A new service:** a keeper deal between a client and a keeper, drafted for the client (Postmark and Longhouse meetings).
- **Admission batches** are billed per clone, whatever the number of members (Backlot meeting).
- **The vow-remedy cMIP is removed** from what we publish: suspended (README-2).
- **The absence-proof cMIP** is co-authored with Postmark, and stays free.
- **Notice** protects clients only, as Keep's list 2 does: a raised price needs 60 days' notice; a lower price or a new service applies once signed.

## Terms of this list

1. **The same for every client.** No discounts, no private rates. Clients who compete are each told in writing that we serve the other.
2. **Every fee is a line in signed terms.** No rulebook or cMIP we publish pays us by default.
3. **Notice for raised prices only.** A raised price applies to new work 60 days after the list raising it is signed, never to work already contracted.
4. **Nothing is drafted for real use before the freeze.** Until then, test identities only.
5. **We never arbitrate, witness or keep the record** in a contest over terms we drafted, for either side.
6. **Every summary we answer for is a signed text act** naming the terms it summarises (README-2, commitment 7).
