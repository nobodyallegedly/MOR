# The Back Row: the member tests, second version

*7 November 2026. Replaces nothing: `member-tests.md` (6 November) stays as it was. What changed, and why:*
- *A fourth mark, from the meetings with both DAOs: "passes, checked by readers" is now told apart from "passes, a signed promise".*
- *An understanding test, agreed with Keep and Postmark: a line shown is not a line understood.*
- *New questions under each test, each from the meeting that raised it: a refund rail on every payment (Hop); the escrow's notice (Hop); the wallet behind money held for members (Hop); a paused pass explained (Hop); a fresh identity by default where several parties are paid, and a bought work kept if it is lost (Pointer, Dubsar); the deletion watch, and size on the clear route (Longhouse); the sliver warning (Counterparty, Pointer); a deposited key that does not open (Postmark).*
- *Where a test's answer waits on a change we ask of the core, it says so, citing `beef-with-the-core.md`.*

We judge a product by what it does to a member, not by what it promises. Each test follows one member, one purchase and one bad day.

## The marks

- **Passes, checked by readers:** a verifier can show from bytes that the member is protected.
- **Passes, a signed promise:** the seller has signed it, but no reader can check it; we print the words "a signed promise, not verifiable".
- **The member carries the risk, and is told:** accepted only when the member chose it and was told in plain words before paying or signing.
- **Fails.**

Each mark names the rule, cMIP or Module it rests on, and who runs it.

## 1. The receipt: who sees that I paid?

- Before I pay, does the screen tell me who can read my purchase, and how many? *(Waits on case 1: which payee the claim sentence means.)*
- Where several parties are paid, is a fresh identity the default, under a lane too?
- Can I choose a fresh identity each period? Where a share of the pool can be sold, is it the default? *(Case 3.)*
- Before paying into a pool whose stake can be sold, am I told: "Anyone who holds part of this pool's stake, now or later, sees how every payment was split"?
- If I pay naming nobody, am I told first that nothing is owed back and I cannot contest?
- Does anything about what I watched leave my device? Does a client's or relay's record name a work, a title or a time?
- On the clear route, am I told that a fetch's size can show what I fetched, and is the routing layer on by default?

## 2. The seal: is what I bought mine?

- Once the key has reached me, can anyone take it back? (The core says no; we check nothing adds a way.)
- If I bought under a fresh identity and lose it, do I keep the work?
- Does my client watch for a deletion notice naming a work I bought, and keep a copy of its locked bytes in time?
- For a pass: is it called access, never a purchase, and does the screen say what this app stops opening when it ends?

## 3. The broken promise: what do I get back?

- Before I pay, am I shown what is owed back, in full and in part, until which block, and who signs it? *(Case 2: today a refund for a promise can only be collected from each holder after payout.)*
- Does every payment name a refund rail, even when no refund is expected?
- Through an escrow: am I told whom I am trusting, and is there a public notice naming the maker for every contested sale?
- For a prepaid period on a bad day: is the unused part owed as a debt a closing cannot shed, and is there money behind it? *(Case 4.)*
- Who holds the keys of the wallet behind any money held for me, and how many must agree to pay?
- If a pass is paused, am I told why, and that nothing was taken?
- Does cancelling end the lane in the same act, and does the screen say a payment already sent stands?
- Where a key was deposited with a keeper and does not open what I paid for, does my client tell me, and offer to publish it?
- Can I contest the record that divided my payment, myself or through a named verifier? *(Case 5.)*

## 4. The lost key: can I recover without handing my keys to anyone?

- Before my first payment, am I told in one sentence that losing my chain key means I can never change keys, change homes or leave?
- Is recovery split among people I choose, never with the company, and am I warned about them acting together and about losing them?
- Was I asked, at setup, whether anyone should take over my name, and told what confirming means?
- **The understanding test.** At least ten people new to MOR, varied in age and in use of technology; right after setup and one week later; open questions in our words, never the seller's; passes at eight in ten, both times. The seller may watch, never prompt. When a question fails, the setup changes, not the person.

## 5. The exit: what leaves with me?

- If I leave this app, seller or DAO: do my identity, receipts, claims, keys to what I bought, period papers, settings and drafts come with me, in the core's own encoding?
- What do I lose, and was I told before I joined?
- If the DAO splits or closes: what do I still hold the next day? *(Case 4.)*
- If my home is caught lying, can I leave at once, and am I told I need my chain key to do it?

## How we report

Each product gets one page: the five tests, each marked, with what the mark rests on. The builder sees it first; we publish it with their answers beside it, and our corrections when we are shown wrong.
