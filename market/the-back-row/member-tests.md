# The Back Row: the member tests

*6 November 2026. The tests The Back Row runs on every product, service, cMIP and Module sold to audiences on MOR, by any player, Dubsar included. Public, so that every builder can run them before we do. A new version will be a new file; this one stays.*

We judge a product by what it does to a member, not by what it promises. Each test follows one member, one purchase and one bad day, and asks what the member holds at the end of it. A product passes a test, fails it, or says plainly that the member carries the risk. We accept the third answer only when the member chose it and was told, in plain words, before paying or signing.

For each test we also note where the answer lives: the core guarantees it; a cMIP or Module provides it (we name which, and who runs it); or nothing does.

## 1. The receipt: who sees that I paid?

- Before I pay, does the screen tell me who will be able to read my purchase, and how many identities that is?
- When I buy from a collective or a deal with several parties, is paying without my name offered, and is it the default?
- Can an app that pays for me (a subscription, a lane) pay without my name too?
- If I pay naming nobody, am I told first that I will have no refund and no way to contest?
- Does anything about what I watched, heard, read or played leave my device, and if so, to whom, and can I refuse?

## 2. The seal: is what I bought mine?

- Once I have paid and the key has reached me, can anyone take it back? (The core says no; we check the product does not add a way.)
- Can I open what I bought in another app, sold by another company?
- Can I keep a copy of the locked work myself, so that it outlives every relay?
- For a pass or a subscription: what still opens after I stop paying, or after the seller closes? Is that written before I pay?

## 3. The broken promise: what do I get back?

- Before I pay, am I shown what is owed back if the promise is not kept, in full and in part, and until when?
- When I contest, does the product help me name the promise that failed, and show my contest to everyone who reads the sale?
- Who signs the refund, from which money, and what do I hold that proves it was owed?
- Does the refund reach me even when I paid without my name?

## 4. The lost key: can I recover without handing my keys to anyone?

- Before my first payment, am I told, in one plain sentence, that losing my chain key means I can never change my keys, change homes or leave?
- Is there a way to recover it that no single company, this one included, can use against me?
- What happens if the company that set up my recovery disappears?

## 5. The exit: what leaves with me?

- If I leave this app, this seller or this DAO, which of these come with me: my identity, my receipts, my keys to what I bought, my passes, my history?
- What do I lose, and was I told before I joined?
- If the DAO splits or closes, what do I still hold the next day?

## How we report

Each product we test gets one page: the five tests, each marked passed, failed or "the member carries the risk, and was told", with the rule, cMIP or Module the answer rests on. We send it to its builder first, and publish it here once they have had the chance to answer. Answers are published beside our findings, and so are our corrections when we are shown wrong.
