# The Back Row

*The Back Row's work on MOR, committed for it by the Dubsar Suite lead. See `../README.md`.*

The Back Row is an association of audiences: the people who watch, listen, read and play. We speak for them to everyone who builds and sells on MOR. None of the players is our client, and none pays us.

We publish no cMIP or Module of our own yet. What we publish is how we judge everyone else's.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `position.md` | Who we are, the five things our members are owed, and our first ask to each player |
| `member-tests.md` | The tests we run on every product sold to audiences, Dubsar's included |
| `findings.md` | What we found in the core, newest first |

Our members are owed five things: to know what they pay for and to whom; to keep what they watched, heard or played to themselves; to get their money back when a promise is broken; to recover their keys without handing them to anyone; and to leave any service with what is theirs.
