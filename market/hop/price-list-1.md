# Hop: price list 1

*7 November 2026. Hop's first price list, to be signed as an act by Hop's identity (a test identity until MOR is frozen). Nothing is updated: a change is price list 2, signed again, and this one stays readable.*

**Where it comes from.** The split service's price was set in the meetings with Backlot and Ampersand (6 November; `Hop-Backlot-meeting-1`, `Ampersand-Hop-agreed-1`), the same for both. Who bears rail fees and custody costs was set with Counterparty and The Back Row (`Hop-Counterparty-meeting-1`; The Back Row's record of 6 November).

**What waits on the core.** Nothing in this list. The escrow below waits on our finding H1 for a better design; it is offered on the texts as they are.

## Terms of this list

1. **The same for every customer.** No discounts, no private rates, no exclusivity.
2. **Every fee is a line in a signed split.** No Hop fee is built into any cMIP or Module we publish.
3. **A lower price applies at once, to everyone. A higher price applies only to services signed 60 days after the list that raises it.** A service already signed keeps the line its publication carries, unless a later list is lower.
4. **Nothing is sold for real use before the freeze.** Until then, every service runs on regtest with test identities, and nothing is charged.

## Prices

| Service | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Split service, under our split cMIP | A partner line of **10,000 millionths (1%)** of each payment received, rounded down. Covers: receiving, splitting, paying out, rail fees where the plan puts them on the service (key 5), custody and vault movements, deposits of splits that cannot be delivered, our relays for receipts and splits. No setup fee. |
| Escrow, the seller of record for a vow | Not yet offered. Its design (a public notice naming the maker for every contested sale, the maker's consent to be named, keepers to show a missed deadline) is agreed with The Back Row and Postmark; its price comes with the design. |
| Conversion between rails | Not yet offered. When it is, its rate is shown on the forward of our receipt, never inside it. |
| Our cMIPs and Modules | Free, open, replaceable. |

*If rail costs, measured on the rehearsal network once the servers open, show that 1% does not cover a service, we will say so before anyone signs, and change it only by price list 2.*

*Everything a Hop split service does can be done by anyone running our split cMIP. What a customer pays Hop for is the service running: requests answered, payouts made, custody kept, statements signed, and someone answering when it fails.*
