## The relationships we cannot do without

**Ampersand and Backlot.** They are our only customers. If either leaves, Hop loses half its revenue; if both leave, Hop has none. We have made leaving them cheap on purpose (terms-1, terms 5 and 8), so the only thing that keeps them is the service. What would make them leave: a measurer that never appears, a pool that does not fit our custody limits with three people, or a wrong split we cannot explain.

**Pointer.** Every split we run starts with a claim from the payer's client. If Pointer's client stopped delivering claims, filled role shares would go unfilled, subscribers' months would link without a fresh identity, and no refund rail would be named. We could not fix any of it from our side. The fallback is any other client implementing the money messages cMIP and Keep's audience-keys Module; today only Dubsar's apps come close, and they are not a product.
