# Postmark

*Postmark's work on MOR, committed for it by the Dubsar Suite lead. See `../README.md`. This folder was `market/notary/` until the firm chose its name.*

**We witness without reading.** Postmark keeps the record of every act around a deal as it arrives, sealed and unread. It anchors that record on a clock of its own, and answers what was recorded and when. It keeps for anyone, on the same terms. It holds no stake in any deal it keeps, judges no contest over one, and gives notice before it stops.
