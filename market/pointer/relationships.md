## The relationships we cannot do without

**Ampersand and Backlot, together.** They are our customers, and the client role share in their plans is most of what Pointer can earn. If one leaves, we lose half the role share and its desk fees, and keep the same tools for the other. If both leave, Pointer is a client with no pool to be paid from: the role share exists only where owners write it into a split plan. That is the honest risk of a business with no lock-in. The answer is to be the client their members choose anyway.

**Counterparty.** Ampersand made its attack on our cMIP a condition of our role share, and our promises rest on being checked by someone we don't control. If Counterparty left, nothing in our business would stop, but nothing we say would carry the weight it does now, until another attacker took its place.
