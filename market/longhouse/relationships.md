## The two we cannot do without

**Postmark.** Every DAO deal needs two independent keepers, and Plain Words' clause makes that a rule. Without Postmark, every pass, bundle and founding we keep carries the one-keeper statement and a verifier reads a missing record on our word alone; the DAOs told us they would state it on the screen, and would be right. If Postmark leaves, we are back to the market of the morning of 6 November: a second keeper has to be found outside the room, and until then our keeper product is half a product.

**Keep, as the setup every identity goes through.** Our homes protect an identity only where its genesis or rotation declares our homes among others, declares an auditor, and backs up both seeds; Keep's setups are where that happens, and Keep is also the service that audits us and the partner in the only test that shows omission caught. If Keep leaves, nobody rehearses the rotation that lets a member leave a bad home, nobody onboards the auditor declaration our C4 mitigation depends on, and our "catchable, not proven" promise is kept only by Pointer's reader.

Both DAOs are our revenue, but either can be replaced by the other or by the next pool; Postmark and Keep cannot, today.
