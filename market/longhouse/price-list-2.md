# Longhouse price list, draft 2

*7 November 2026. Rehearsal terms, regtest. For `market/longhouse/price-list-2.md`. Read with `terms-2.md`. Price list 1 stays. Every price is public and the same for everyone; every fee is a line in a signed term or split, none hidden in a rate.*

| Service | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Home, one person | 300 sat / period |
| Home, a collective | 1,500 sat / period |
| Auditing relay | 1,000 sat / period per home audited |
| Keeper, per person, every agreement naming Longhouse | 2,000 sat / period + 5 sat / record |
| Keeper, per collective, every agreement it is party to | 2,000 sat / period + 5 sat / record |
| Anchoring service | 200 sat / anchor + the chain fee |
| Carrying and keeping ordinary acts | 100 sat per 1,000 acts / period |
| Delivery records | 0; on a sale of one work, paid by the role share the publication offers; nothing under a pass |
| Storage, under 1 TB | 400 sat / GB / period |
| Storage, 1 TB and up | 300 sat / GB / period |
| Storage, 10 TB and up | 220 sat / GB / period |
| Storage, 100 TB and up | 160 sat / GB / period |

A period is 4,320 blocks on the agreement's time reference. Storage tiers are reached by a single customer's volume or a pool's, alike. A bake whose term ends is announced by a signed notice 4,320 blocks before its bytes go; anyone may take over keeping it at the storage price. Leaving, and the hand-over of everything a customer owns, costs nothing. A customer-chosen auditor costs what that auditor charges and Longhouse nothing extra.
