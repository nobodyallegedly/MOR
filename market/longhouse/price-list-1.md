# Longhouse price list, draft 1

*6 November 2026. Rehearsal terms, regtest. For `market/longhouse/price-list-1.md`. Every price is public and the same for everyone; every fee is a line in a signed term or split, none hidden in a rate. Read with `terms-1.md`. Not part of the core.*

| Service | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Home, one person | 300 sat / month |
| Home, a collective | 1,500 sat / month |
| Auditing relay | 1,000 sat / month per home audited |
| Keeper, per person, every agreement naming Longhouse | 2,000 sat / month + 5 sat / record |
| Keeper, per collective, every agreement it is party to | 2,000 sat / month + 5 sat / record |
| Clock, anchoring a keeper's sequence | 200 sat / anchor + the chain fee |
| Delivery records | 0; paid by the role share a publication offers, when the payer acknowledges the record |
| Storage, under 1 TB | 400 sat / GB / month |
| Storage, 1 TB and up | 300 sat / GB / month |
| Storage, 10 TB and up | 220 sat / GB / month |
| Storage, 100 TB and up | 160 sat / GB / month |

Storage tiers are reached by a single customer's volume or a pool's, alike; a pool's volume counts for a member while the pool's counting act stands (terms-1, Storage). Leaving, and the hand-over of everything a customer owns, costs nothing.
