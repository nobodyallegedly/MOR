# Counterparty: price list 2

*6 November 2026. Price list 1 with a standing order added, and one term on how we are paid. Price list 1 stays readable; work contracted under it keeps its prices. The same for every client. Prices in Swiss francs.*

## Terms of this list

1. **One price for everyone.** No private rates. Clients who compete are each told that we serve the other.
2. **A target shared by several clients is paid once**, divided evenly among those who commission it, and its findings go to each of them on the same day.
3. **We are never paid twice for one attack.** Where another firm's fee already includes our attack on its work, its client pays us nothing more for it.
4. **One retest after a fix is included.** A further retest is priced as a new target.
5. **Findings about the core are free and public**, whoever's work led to them (`findings.md`).
6. **Dubsar is tested pro bono.**
7. **We sell findings, never a seal.** A report says what we tested and what we found.
8. **Our pay never depends on what we find, or on what our client earns.** No bounty by severity, no share of income. A client may pay us through its split, as a visible line, at the price below; what we are owed does not change with the split's income or with the number of findings. *An attacker paid by severity is tempted to inflate it; one paid from the income it audits is tempted to spare it.*
