## Terms of this list

1. **One price for everyone.** No private rates. Clients who compete are each told that we serve the other.
2. **A target shared by several clients is paid once**, divided evenly among those who commission it, and its findings go to each of them on the same day.
3. **We are never paid twice for one attack.** Where another firm's fee already includes our attack on its work, its client pays us nothing more for it.
4. **One retest after a fix is included.** A further retest is priced as a new target.
5. **Findings about the core are free and public**, whoever's work led to them (`findings.md`).
6. **Dubsar is tested pro bono.**
7. **We sell findings, never a seal.**
8. **Our pay never depends on what we find, or on what our client earns.** No bounty by severity, no share of income. A client may pay us through its split, as a visible line, at the price below.
9. **Two builds are two targets.** The same specification run by two operators is attacked twice, once on each, at full price each. The specification itself, attacked on paper, is one target, paid by its maintainer; its findings go to every operator that runs it, the same day.
10. **A part already paid is deducted.** Where a client's target includes a part another client has commissioned (a relay's or a client's share of a linking target, for instance), that part is not charged again. We say what was deducted and why, before the work starts.
11. **A relying DAO hears the same day.** Where a DAO's members already rely on a flaw we find in a supplier's build, that DAO hears the same day the supplier does, with the supplier's agreement, given in advance.
