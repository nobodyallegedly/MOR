## The relationships we cannot do without

**The Back Row.** Our product is findings people believe. A client cannot certify our honesty, and the core cannot either; only a reader with no stake in our clients who has promised to publish the line that shows we were bought. If The Back Row left, or stopped reading us, our reports would carry only our own word, and "quality is marketing" would have no one to check the quality. We would ask another association to take its place, and say publicly that the seat was empty until one did.

**Ampersand.** It is our largest engagement, and the client whose founding date now depends on our findings (C12, C14). If it does not found, we lose most of 20,250 and the live work on the ceremony, the measurer and the pool. Nothing we published is lost: the core findings stand, and Backlot's pool faces the same ones. What we would lose is the first real pool to attack end to end, which is the reference every later client will be compared with.
