# Keep: price list 2

*6 November 2026. Published by Keep, to be signed as an act by Keep's identity (a test identity until MOR is frozen). Price list 1 stays readable; every service contracted under it keeps the fee its signed terms carry.*

## What changed from list 1

- Two new services: a small collective setup, and an audience support line.
- The notice rule now protects customers only: a **raised** price needs 60 days' notice; a lower price or a new service applies once signed.

## Terms of this list

1. **The same for every customer.** No discounts, no private rates.
2. **Every fee is a line in signed terms.** No Keep fee is built into any rulebook we publish.
3. **Notice for raised prices only.** A price raised by a later list applies to new services 60 days after that list is signed. A lower price, or a new service, applies once signed. No list ever reaches a service already contracted: that service keeps the fee its signed terms carry.
4. **Nothing is sold for real use before the freeze.** Until then, every service runs on a test network with test identities.
