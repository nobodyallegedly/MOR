## Terms of this list

1. **The same for every customer.** No discounts, no private rates.
2. **Every fee is a line in signed terms.** No Keep fee is built into any rulebook we publish.
3. **Notice for raised prices only.** A price raised by a later list applies to new services 60 days after that list is signed. A lower price, or a new service, applies once signed. No list ever reaches a service already contracted.
4. **Nothing is sold for real use before the freeze.** Until then, every service runs on a test network with test identities.
5. **We never hold a key, a share of one, or a recovery secret in a form we could use**, whatever the service.
