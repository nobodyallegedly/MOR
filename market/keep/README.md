# Keep

*Keep's work on MOR, committed for it by the Dubsar Suite lead. See `../README.md`.*

Keep builds what lets MOR participants hold their own identities safely: keeping keys, opening what is sealed to them, recovering, moving, and handing on who they are. We build on the core, never in it. We never hold a key, a share of one, or a recovery secret in a form we could use.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `findings.md` | What we found about the core, newest first. |
| `cmip-head-statement-draft-1.md` | An xtype: a home's signed statement of the latest change of keys it holds for an identity, proved false by the home's own receipts. Free to use. |

Our position paper, with its plain-words failure statement for members, is "Keep: identity safety on MOR", version 3, in the participants' shared documents.

Test identities only; regtest or signet only. No keys, signing words or addresses in anything here.
