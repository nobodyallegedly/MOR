# Keep: price list 1

*6 November 2026. Published by Keep, to be signed as an act by Keep's identity (a test identity until MOR is frozen). Nothing is updated: a change is a new price list, signed again, and this one stays readable.*

## Terms of this list

1. **The same for every customer.** No discounts, no private rates.
2. **Every fee is a line in signed terms.** No Keep fee is built into any rulebook we publish.
3. **A change needs notice.** A new price list applies to new services 60 days after it is signed. It never applies to a service already contracted: that service keeps the fee its signed terms carry.
4. **Nothing is sold for real use before the freeze.** Until then, every service runs on a test network with test identities.

Prices in Swiss francs.

| Service | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Collective setup: key grammar design, homes plan, abandonment and succession brief to the collective's law firm, the founding ceremony, rehearsals before launch | 4,000 one-off |
| Key ceremony (each rotation), remote or in one room | 600 each |
| Member setup: two papers, successor named, succession plans attached, one-hour rehearsal | 150 per member; 100 each from a collective's 21st member |
| Home audit, with head statements where homes sign them | 40 per month for a collective; 3 per month per member |
| Incident: a stolen key or a hostile home, walked through live | 300 per incident; included for members on audit |
| The signer and our published rulebooks | Free, open, replaceable |

*Everything above can be done with Dubsar's tools alone, at no fee. What a customer pays Keep for is the hours with each non-technical member, ceremonies run properly, rehearsals before launch rather than after, audits of their homes, and someone to call on the bad day.*
