For testing only. Test identities only, until MOR is frozen; regtest or signet only. The risks, in plain words:

  1. Whoever serves this page’s code at the moment you bring an identity here could copy its signing key: with it, sign as you (proposals, signatures, a collective’s acts) and read its private messages.
  2. This browser’s storage keeps, as they are and not locked: the signing and encryption keys of each identity you bring; the signing key of each collective you found here; and, while you sign as a member, the one-time key for your share until you have written the share down. That storage can be lost (cleared, a private window, the browser tidying up), or read by what runs in this page.
  3. Founding a collective here deals its chain key in this page: for a moment this page holds the whole chain key, in its memory, never in its storage; your share and any shown hand to hand wait there, an hour at most, until shown, and a reload or a closed tab loses them. If this page’s code or this device were not honest, it could keep the chain key and later act alone (F337). For a collective that matters, found it in Edubba on a computer.
  4. No agents, no API key and nothing from Claude here: those need Edubba on the Mac.

Never bring a real identity here.
