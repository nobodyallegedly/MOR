# Clients

The TypeScript clients (build brief: "Rust for the core library, relays and homes; TypeScript for clients"). Each uses the core library through WebAssembly (`wasm/`, crate `mor-wasm`), so every act is made and judged by the same Rust code as the relays; a client only fetches, stores and shows.

| Folder | What | Roadmap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `genesis/` | The genesis client: test identities, routes, encryption keys, rotations, key delivery. | step 5 |
| `repo/` | The repo client: a test collective under a founding agreement, releases of the code as signed manifests, verification of every file. | step 5a |
| `longform/` | The long-form text format: reading, rendering to HTML with the plain text one tap away, and the Text MIR's bound checked on every rendering; a document as a text act, published and read back verified. | step 8 |
| `barebone/` | The barebone client: a post with a picture (a text act referring to a JPEG publication, stripped to the picture alone), shown verified; withdrawal. | step 9 |
| `dub/` | Dub (called MOR Post until 13 October 2026; reshaped 16 October 2026, F332): an app on the Mac, no command line, and the same page as a web client at dub.dubsar.org, for testing only (F281). An identity window first (make one, its chain key's words shown once and kept nowhere, hidden before three are asked back; bring one by its signing key's words, which carry its encryption key too; switch between those held; a display name, the naming cMIP; the signing key's words on request); then two tabs: **Public** (the feed of its own posts and the followed, in signed order; post with a picture, optionally to an identity; reply, follow and mute on every post; follow by ID, the people cMIP; a "to you" filter with a count; long-form documents as a link to the reader) and **Private** (the inbox, messages sealed to one identity, a count). No rotation: when its key is changed elsewhere (MOR Safety), it says so and takes the new words. The desk's server and launcher, the site's feed. | step 9b; F332 |
| `reader/` | The web reader, the door: opens by link, verifies in the browser, renders documents and posts, shows the signer's fingerprint; reach the owner by email or by a message sealed over MOR. | step 10 |
| `site/` | Websites on MOR: a site as a signed manifest of its files; a gateway serving it at an address, whose display client checks every page in the visitor's browser and shows who signed it; publish, verify and check from the command line. | step 10a |
| `manage/` | The management page for relays and homes, served by the relay itself at `/manage/`: a browser paired once with a one-time code signs every request; approvals, lists, a limit on new identities, the operator's rotation. | step 11 |
| `kishib/` | **Kišib** (F335; F337), the one place where a chain key is used, from paper, and then forgotten: an app on the Mac and Linux and the same page as a web client, for testing only (to be served at kishib.dubsar.org). Change an identity's keys from its chain key's paper words, keeping every line of its acts, resending the same bytes, the new signing words shown once; rotate a collective from its members' paper shares, completing a member change proposed in Edubba (remote with a one-time key that expires after an hour, or in the same room); escape a contested position with both keys; name or confirm a successor; check paper words; an identity's record. It holds nothing between uses. | F335, F337 |
| `damgar/` | **Damgar** (F338), the money app that holds no wallet: an app on the Mac and Linux and the same page as a web client, for testing only (to be served at damgar.dubsar.org). An identity brought by its signing words; the cMIPs and Modules accepted, sealed to the identity (F340); **To sign**: drafts from Dub and Edubba sealed to oneself, requests to be paid, invoices to make, receipts to sign, each in plain words with its rail; **Pay**: the invoice or an on-chain address bound to the payment, paid in one's own wallet, the proof pasted back; **The account**, each claim and receipt verified from the rail's evidence, totals per unit; **Settings**: where one is paid, with each rail's costs, and the on-chain request key. Test networks only. | F338, F340 |
| `ummia/` | **Ummia** (F339), the developers' app, the Development layer's: an app on the Mac and Linux and the same page as a web client, for testing only (to be served at ummia.dubsar.org). An identity brought by its signing words; who acts (a shared chooser: oneself in round 1, collectives in round 2); releases of code under one's own name (moved from Edubba), checked as anyone would, anchored on Bitcoin as an option (paid in Damgar); cMIPs and Modules published as acts, their hash shown first; tracking cMIPs, Modules, extensions and xtypes on the public relays one names, with the filters relays have (public agreements only, said); MIP drafts as long-form documents, then the MIP as a specification once ready; bug reports and their answers; the whole accepted list (F340), changed and merged. | F339, F340 |
| `testwallet/` | A test wallet, regtest only: a plain page over the regtest lnd nodes, to pay and be paid while trying Damgar. Not part of MOR. | F338 |
| `anchoring-service/` | A test pooled anchoring service on regtest: its standing offer, tickets, receipts and published batches, talking only through MOR acts. | F338 |
| `collective/` | The collective client, taken into Edubba on 16 October 2026 (F333): its page retired as the person's app, its code the library Edubba's Collectives tab uses (its operations, its page logic mounted as the Constitution sub-tab, acting for a collective with the permissions its agreement gives); releases under one's own name, founding a collective, adding and removing members, signing and verifying releases, leaving; every step read back in plain words from the exact bytes before it is signed. | step 11b; F333 |
| `connector/` | The MOR connector for Claude, an MCP server: reads and verifies acts by id or link, says in plain words who signed what and whether it counts (in force as far as the relays asked show); prepares posts, pictures, withdrawals and messages as drafts for Edubba, never an Agreements act; it never holds a key nor sends an act. Drafts are files in `~/mor-drafts`, whose format `connector/DRAFTS.md` documents. Installed outside `~/Documents` by `npm run add-to-claude`, since Claude's app may not start a program kept there. | steps 11a, 11c |
| `desk/` | **Edubba** (called MOR Identities until 16 October 2026, F333), the tablet house: a program on the owner's Mac with a page in the browser, no command line, in Dub's look, three tabs, each a list on top and the one selected below. **Agents**: a first-use window (the API key, kept owner-only and shown by its last four characters; the agent's identity and published display name; its terms, shown then signed), + Add an agent, each agent's terms, queue, nudge, daily cap, shared or own key, drafts from Claude, and a prompt box whose words are a signed private act answered at once; MOR as seen by each agent (its follows, read under a nudge). **Identities**: the others, drafts from Claude approved, declined or sent back, what each received, sorted. **Collectives**: what you may do for each, from its agreement, each permission naming its rule; its constitution in plain words; the collective client's work. Reads `~/mor-desk` and `~/mor-collective` where they are. | step 11c; F333 |
| `assess/` | The deal-assessment tool: explains an agreement in plain words (who signed, what each party is bound to, the shares and fees, who judges and in what order, how it ends, what is still owed) and flags its obvious risks, each naming the rule or stated cost it rests on. Shared code: the command `mor-assess ID --at URL`, the collective client's readings before signing, and the desk's received terms. Every fact is the core library's. | step 14 |
| `nudge/` | The nudge cMIP (draft 2): an agent's agency terms, the nudge and the pick, made and read; beside an action, the reading anyone can check (within its terms, free or by pick; outside; unknown; unclear; over the count). Used by Edubba for each of its agents. | after the rename pass |
| `reports/` | Reports on MOR mirrored on GitHub, both ways (F287): a command run by hand on the author's computer reads the reporting identity's public reports (each from its reporter's own identity) and its answers, checks each, and opens or updates one GitHub issue per report, each answer a comment; for the issues opened on GitHub that the author names after its dry run, prepares the reporting identity's re-publication as a draft for Edubba, which signs it on approval, and copies answers back. A private report is never mirrored; nothing is signed by the command; the token never printed. | F287 |

Building any of them needs Node 22 or later, Rust with the `wasm32-unknown-unknown` target, and `wasm-bindgen-cli` of the version the `mor-wasm` crate pins:

```
rustup target add wasm32-unknown-unknown
cargo install wasm-bindgen-cli --version 0.2.129 --locked
```
