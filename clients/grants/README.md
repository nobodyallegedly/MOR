# mor-grants

Grants between identities, as a shared piece (decided by Nobody, allegedly, 17 October 2026, "an example of grant": "Build it as a shared piece: Damgar's round 2 lanes (grants to apps) will reuse it"). TypeScript, with the core library through WebAssembly; nothing from Node, so the web clients can use it (Dub's does).

**Test identities only, until MOR is frozen.**

## In plain words

A grant lets someone act in your name, within limits, with a key of their own (Agreements, F128: "Grant keys for every identity"). You sign the grant, public, saying who may act, what it may sign, how much one act may move and until when. The grantee makes the key, and signs to accept. What it then signs with that key is your own act, naming the grant, so every reader sees who signed it and on whose authority. One revocation ends it.

Relays and homes store an act signed under a grant as scoped (relay transport cMIP draft 5, F346; `relay/src/node.rs`, `check_binding`).

`authorityOf` judges a private act's limits from its inside, opened with its key (round 2: a sealed claim above the cap was read as within it, judged from its outside alone). Its words for a lane: "Signed under a lane of … (a grant to itself, …)".

**A deal between separate people** (`src/deal.ts`, 23 October 2026, moved to the redesigned core at F407). In plain words: who owns the work and who sells it are now two documents. The **ownership agreement** says only who owns what share; the **publication** says how it is sold, which split service receives every payment and pays everyone, and what comes off the top for a reposter. The deal's own payee and its grant are gone: the split service is paid directly, through the price tag it signs.

Precisely: a claim (`claimPayload`, Agreements type 3); the work's ownership agreement on it (`dealTermsPayload`, terms field 33 `[0, claim]`, one stake in field 7; no fields 8, 14, 28, 29) and, for a vow, the clone sharing its stake (rule 14, `dealClonePayload`); the publication (`dealPublicationPayload`, type 6, its owners the agreement, its objects `[agreement, agreement]`, `dealPublicationObjects`; read back by `dealPublicationRead`), checked by the core; a deal read (`readDeal`: parties, owners and shares, the claim, and with a publication its split service, who is paid, and the referral); the deal a post is under, from an ownership agreement or a publication its refs name (`dealOfPost`; the standing offer, `AGREEMENTS_TYPES.offer`, is gone); the one line said before paying. `authorityOf` no longer looks for deals behind a payee's grant: the payee's grant in a deal (terms fields 14 and 29, "this agreement" in grant field 2) is retired with F407.

## Readings

1. **A person's grant offered to an agent** reaches Text posts, Envelopes bakes (uploads), or the Money layer, layer 3 since Text and Envelopes became two layers (`GRANTABLE`); scope 2.
2. **A cap applies to the amount a Money act names**, and to an invoice request's (the money messages cMIP, its commitment's amount, round 2); an act naming none moves nothing.
3. **An expiry on a timestamp clock** is judged by the earliest verified stamp of that clock naming the act; with none, unknown.
