## The line as data

Written in `core/src/agreements/control.rs`'s documentation; rule 99's "the collective's line at the act", spelled out. The core computes, at the line that would put the act or clone in force, and hands the model as plain data, never a callback:

- the voices that remain;
- each stake's roster, field 7 with the placed transfers applied;
- the signature acts the line places on the act judged, by signer;
- every act of the model's own specification the line placed (offer, take-up, proxy, revocation), with its signer, payload and place;
- the facts the caller states: payments toward an act; acts within a period on the time reference; lapsed periods.

The code does not follow it yet: `view/issuance.rs` (296 lines) and `view/proxies.rs` (75 lines) still call about 25 private view methods. Turning them into pure functions of this data is the next step.
