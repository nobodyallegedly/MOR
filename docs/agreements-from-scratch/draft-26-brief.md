**The words used for each rule.**
- **STAYS**: no standing entry changes what the rule says. Where an entry names the rule and keeps or confirms it, the row says so; otherwise the row reads "stays as it is" and its other cells are empty.
- **GOES**: the rule leaves and is written nowhere else.

- **CHANGES**: the rule stays in Agreements and a standing entry changes what it says. What changes is said in plain words; no new wording is written.
