## What P8 changed

**In plain words.** Draft 26 was written for agreements with versions. Nobody, allegedly, dropped them on 28 November. Now an agreement has one set of Terms, which nothing replaces. It lives until its ending condition is met: by signatures, or by anything the bytes can check, a clock among them. Any change is that end plus a new agreement. The new agreement's Terms may carry a closing pointer saying the old one is no longer valid and that it once existed. The pointer closes the old one only where its signatures meet the old one's ending condition.

A keeper is a relay where the agreement's acts are: the core asks of it only what it asks of any relay, and it gives no order. Acts are ordered by their knots. Answers like "not ended" are given as far as the acts held show. Those who join through a spot stay bound by their Terms, and the agreement is not dissolved while something is still owed to them. What an agreement does is always a cMIP's answer. Draft 26, encoding 3 and the companion now say so.
