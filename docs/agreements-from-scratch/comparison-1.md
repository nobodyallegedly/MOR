## In plain words

Almost everything the old core guarded is either a rule of draft 1 or something a cMIP can supply through its existing tasks. **The losses cluster in one place: the way out.** Draft 1 has no default way out. Leaving, ending, a member's exit and a principal's dismissal of an agent all work only if the parties wrote them in advance. A cMIP can supply them through rule 4's powers and task K4, but only for those who chose that cMIP; it cannot protect someone whose terms said nothing. Draft 1 states this openly as a cost and chooses it deliberately.

Three smaller things have no home: what is owed back when a payment bought nothing, the client duties, and the absence choices decided on 11 November (parked, not carried into this stint).

## What cMIPs can and cannot fill

**Filled by existing tasks.** Ten guarantees need no new task: K4 (a rule anyone can recompute) carries splits, shares, waiting periods, the chain of judges and which changes need whom; K5, K10 and K11 carry absence, contests and what is owed back. Draft 1's rule 4 makes all of them binding only where the parties signed them.

**Not fillable by a cMIP alone.** The four losses are all defaults: what holds when the terms say nothing. A cMIP is chosen by those who sign; a default protects those who did not think to choose. By its own test ("whom does it protect who did not choose it?") a default way out would pass both questions, which is why draft 1 had to write its absence as a cost.

## For Nobody, allegedly

Not a question list; the two places where the comparison touches his own words:

- **The way out.** The project's instructions: "MOR is built to be a good ancestor. The way out (succession, portable identities, agreements that can be cloned across, anchored history) must be right." Draft 1 chooses "designed in advance or not at all". The losses S6, C2, C3 and C5, and the open P3, are one question about the way out.
- **Client duties.** On 11 November: "I think forcing clients to behave a certain healthy way is good"; "It creates something that can be used to criticized lazy or malicious clients". Draft 1 has none.
