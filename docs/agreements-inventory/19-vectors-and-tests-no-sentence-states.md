Row ids are from the rows of this inventory (draft 24). This file decides nothing. Where a verdict is "a" but part of what the test checks has no sentence behind it, the row says so, and that part is listed again under "Laws the text never stated".

Verdicts: (a) exercises a sentence of draft 24; (b) another MIR; (c) a law no normal sentence of draft 24 states; (d) history or a superseded state.

## Laws the text never stated

1. **Verdicts do not depend on the order in which acts arrive.** A verifier that receives the same acts in any order, even if it is asked for answers while they are still arriving, gives every verdict the same. Tests: `deal_verdicts_do_not_depend_on_order`, `owners_verdicts_do_not_depend_on_order`, `ic6_...`, and the replay inside the invariants.
