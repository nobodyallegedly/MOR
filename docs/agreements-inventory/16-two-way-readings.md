# Agreements draft 24, inventory: sentences that read two ways

*Two lists. First, the six readings a stranger named (`docs/reviews/fable-fresh-eyes.md`, pull request 134, section 2), each tied to the rows that hold it in draft 24, where draft 24 holds it. Then every other row where a worker of this inventory noted that a careful stranger could read the sentence two ways, as noted. Nothing here says which reading is right.*

## The stranger's six

1. **Which abandonment clause judges someone who is no longer a member.** Rule 85, "A declaration against a holder who is no party is judged under the abandonment clause of the version that holder last signed as a party": [A-1666](10-part-4b.md) (line 2219). Rule 87, "a party whose voice was removed before a judicial change is judged by the clause in force": [A-1699](10-part-4b.md) (line 2257), and the abandonment declaration's format, which repeats it: [A-2395](14-part-5c.md) (line 3312). A member who resigned is both a departed holder who is no party and a party whose voice was removed; neither sentence says which wins. The worker for rule 87 noted that F330 (Q2) recorded a different reading of a related case (an outside holder with a stake successor) from the one draft 24 now writes; that is recorded, not judged, here.
2. **How many outcomes absence has.** "A vanished party's two outcomes" in the outline, the last resort and rule 48: [A-016](01-front.md) (line 23), [A-1443](09-part-4a.md) (line 1949), [A-1078](06-part-2b.md) (line 1462). Rule 89 lists four, outcome 2 struck: [A-1710](10-part-4b.md) (line 2279) and the format [A-1957](12-part-5a.md) (line 2671). The likelier meaning (the two that move a stake) is not written.
3. **Whether a deal can change without everyone.** The stranger's quotation is from the MICS glossary. In draft 24 the same tension sits between the glossary's "A deal's end" ("a clone every party signs") [A-162](01-front.md) (line 170) and the body's "save a terminating act under its termination rule": [A-1442](09-part-4a.md) (line 1948), [A-1497](09-part-4a.md) (line 1996), [A-306](03-part-1b.md) (line 373), [A-2096](12-part-5a.md) (line 2878). Draft 24's own glossary entry "Deal" [A-045](01-front.md) (line 59) says nothing about change.
4. **How often a chain key signs.** The MICS and Identity only; draft 24 does not echo "used exactly once". No row.
5. **What "pending" means.** "*Pending* has one meaning" is the MICS's sentence, not draft 24's. Draft 24 uses the word for: a payment waiting on the split service's receipt [A-373](03-part-1b.md) (line 430); receipts waiting on the rail's answer [A-383](03-part-1b.md) (line 440); a vow's sale neither confirmed nor contested [A-400](03-part-1b.md) (line 466), [A-069](01-front.md) (line 83); condition evaluation's answer, which does not pass the chain of judgment [A-1745](11-part-4c.md) (line 2339), [A-1773](11-part-4c.md) (line 2380), and termination's "not yet met" read as it [A-1788](11-part-4c.md) (line 2386).
6. **How a collective ends.** Rule 91's title, "four shapes" (a heading, no row; noted on [A-1265](08-part-3b.md) (line 1712)): shape A [A-1267](08-part-3b.md) (line 1716) and shape C [A-1311](08-part-3b.md) (line 1747) end nothing; "A fork closes the original" is italic in rule 91 and a reasoning title in Part six [A-2583](15-part-6.md) (line 3558), and holds for shape B only [A-1273](08-part-3b.md) (line 1718).
