# MOR companion documents

Companion documents are written for readers outside the project. They explain MOR; they are not part of the core and add no rule. The MICS, its document and its six MIRs in `spec/`, defines the protocol.

- [The unwritten layer](05-MOR-the-unwritten-layer-v3.md): the economies people build on the six layers, in the shape of the third film: the economy we know made honest, eight others, and the one that rewards everyone along the pipe; the redraft of [Who Can Earn on MOR](05-MOR-who-can-earn-v6.md), version 6, which stays.
- [Core principle: Don't ban greed, make it legible](Core-principle-legible-greed-v3.md): why the core never judges whether a deal is fair, and what it guarantees instead.

"MOR in one page" is at [`docs/07-MOR-in-one-page-v11.md`](../07-MOR-in-one-page-v11.md) (version 11: the author's redraft, version 10, with the layers' new names; not yet approved). The case studies are in [`../case-studies/`](../case-studies/README.md).

*These are working drafts. The documents that are neither core nor technical companions get a final draft by Nobody, allegedly, before publication.*
