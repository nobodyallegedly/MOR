# What Nobody, allegedly, and the MOR lead arrived at

*This file records what Nobody, allegedly, and the MOR lead arrived at together, and nothing else counts as his direction. He said, on 12 November: "My words matter less than our words within that conversation do." So each entry records the exchange: what the MOR lead (or Fable, or a session) asked or proposed, labelled as theirs; his words, quoted exactly; and one line of what was arrived at, written by the MOR lead and marked "to check" until he has read it. Each stint opens with his wish, kept at the top for the whole stint. A new wish starts a new stint; the old one moves to the history below, and nothing from it carries over unless he says so. Everything else in the repository (findings, reviews, prompts, reports) is a proposal or a record of work, labelled with whose it is.*

*How his words are read: as one train of thought, later answers refining earlier ones; never one isolated sentence given more weight than the exchange gives it ("Be mindful of taking isolated sentences out of context and give them too much weight", 12 November).*

**A later version and a closed spot** (draft 6's open list). The MOR lead asked whether a later version could undo a closed spot or remove fillings already earned (an offer's author signing that earlier licences no longer count). He: "Whoever paid the agreement has the rights the agreement offer."
- Then, on whether that version can itself limit the rights: "That version could have conditions tied to time, creators statements… bla bla bla… core does not care"
- *Arrived at (to check):* a filling earned under a version has exactly the rights that version offers, conditions included (a time limit, a creator's statement, anything its cMIPs read); the core does not care what they are. A version may name the rule (a cMIP) under which its rights end; given the facts, a verifier answers **void**: a state, whatever the cause (a time, a statement), the cause shown beside it; **invalid** stays for an act that never counted. No later version changes them without the holder's own signature. Then: "Exactly. So the agreement should have a say to give a verifier the ingredients to provide VOID as a reply, no?" The MOR lead distinguished invalid (the act never counted) from void (it counted, and its rights ended by its own version's conditions), and asked whether "void" or "lapsed" is the word. He: "Yes, that distinction matters. Lapsed feels associated to time. Void to state" Reopening a closed spot to new identities is a new version under the Terms' own rule, and touches no earned filling.

- *Arrived at (to check):* an act's "now" is what it cites (the versions and acts it names); order emerges from the knots where chains cite each other, with no clock imposed; every act should name what it relied on (so a payment names the work's version it read, which also covers the fork case of option A); for "nothing later" and for time, each accepts the anchoring it is willing to accept.
