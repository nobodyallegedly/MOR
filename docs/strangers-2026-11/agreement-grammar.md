# The grammar test

10 Nov 2026. Companion to `agreement-tasks.md`.

## The hypothesis

The 49 tasks are questions. The same few operations keep answering them. If so, the 49 are a checklist of what can go wrong, and the operations are the toolkit one would actually build.

**Five operations**

1. **State**: fix a set of words so they cannot shift.
2. **Sign**: someone identifiable binds themselves to those words.
3. **Deliver**: get the signed words in front of a particular person, or everyone.
4. **Order**: place them in a shared sequence, so "before", "after" and "latest" have an answer.
5. **Control**: have power over a thing, so it moves only when a condition is met.

**Grammar**: *who* does *which operation* on *what*, *when*.

- Who: a party, a newcomer, an agent, a group, an outsider.
- What: the deal, a rule, a fact about the world, a holding, a status.

## The test: all 49, one at a time

Verdicts:

- **Clean**: fully expressed by the five operations.
- **Part**: clean in one case, not in another.
- **Witness**: can be expressed, but only as an outsider's signed statement, which proves they said it, not that it is true.
- **Mind**: concerns what someone understood, wanted or knows. No operation reaches it.
- **Hand**: needs force over something not already under Control.
- **Property**: not an operation at all, but a quality every operation must have.

## Result

| Verdict | Count | Tasks |
|---------|-------|-------|
| Clean | 33 | (all others) |
| Part | 4 | Due, Count, Whole, Alive |
| Witness | 5 | Identify, Perform, Inspect, Measure, Judge |

The hypothesis mostly holds: 33 of 49 are fully expressed by five operations, and 4 more in their simpler case.

## Findings

**1. Keep and Check are not tasks.** They are two qualities every operation must have: its result must last (durable) and an outsider must be able to test it (verifiable). The toolkit is five operations times two qualities. They should probably leave the task list and become requirements on it.

**2. There is a silent sixth operation: Apply.** Sum, Divide, Count and Wait all involve applying a stated rule to known inputs. It was hidden because it needs no trust: anyone can redo the calculation. That is exactly why it matters. Whatever can be turned into Apply stops needing an outsider.

**3. One rule turns absences into lookups.** Current, Whole and half of Alive ask to prove that nothing else exists. They become clean only under a closed-world rule: "what is not in the register does not count". This is a design decision, not a discovery, and it is what land registers and company registers do. Where the rule cannot apply (Conceal: nobody can register a non-telling), the absence stays unprovable.

**4. The residue is three things, in this order of size.**

- **Truth** (5 tasks fully, 3 in part): Identify, Perform, Inspect, Measure, Judge, and the harder half of Due, Alive, Count. Whenever the agreement touches the world outside the record, someone must say what happened, and their signature proves only that they said it.
- **Minds** (4): Able, Align, Free, Conceal. What someone understood, wanted or knows.
- **Hands** (1, but it sits under every use of Control): Enforce. Words can order a release; something must obey.

**5. The grammar handles words and held value well, and deeds badly.** A payment from held value is clean. A service rendered is a fact about the world. The more an agreement is about deeds, the more it leans on witnesses.

## Design consequences

- Convert Witness tasks into Apply wherever possible: choose terms that can be computed from the record itself instead of observed in the world.
- Convert Hand into Control by posting value in advance.
- Adopt the closed-world rule so that absences become lookups.
- For what remains under Truth, use several witnesses, so none can lie alone.
- For Minds, do not try to prove; assign in advance who bears the failure.

## Second test: no single shared sequence

Supposition: there is no single Order. Each person keeps their own sequence, chained so that each entry points at the one before it. There are witnesses.

Twelve tasks change. They fall in two clusters: "only once" and "by when".

| Task | Was | Now | Why |
|------|-----|-----|-----|
| Order | Clean | Part | Still clean inside one sequence, and wherever one entry points at another. Between unrelated entries, only a witness can say. |
| Title | Clean | Witness | The same thing can be given twice in two branches, and nothing breaks the tie. |
| Transfer | Clean | Witness | Same. |
| Sum | Clean | Witness | Nobody can see all the parts at once. |
| Leave | Clean | Part | The Divide route survives; the Transfer route does not. |
| Current | Clean | Witness | Whoever shows a sequence can stop before its latest entry. |
| Notify | Clean | Part | Clean if the receiver acknowledges. "Published, so deemed told" is gone. |
| Wait | Clean | Witness | Silence in whose sequence, counted from when? |
| Due | Part | Witness | Anyone can backdate their own entries. |
| Hear | Clean | Part | Inherits Wait. |
| Succeed | Clean | Part | Which will is the latest? The author can no longer be asked. |
| Count | Part | Witness | Who was a member at the moment of the vote needs an order across sequences. |

Clean falls from 33 to 23.

Two keep their verdict for a new reason:

- **Whole** stays Part, but improves. In a chained sequence a missing middle entry is detectable. Only the end can be hidden, which is the Current problem.
- **Alive** stays Part. Presence is still clean: hand over a fresh challenge and they sign it.

What survives untouched: Fix, Commit, Exchange, Bind, Acknowledge, Authorise, Record, Amend, Complete. An amendment needs both signatures, so neither party can be surprised by one. Only a stranger arriving later is exposed.

**Findings**

- A shared sequence is needed for exactly two things: **scarcity** (this thing, to one person only) and **freshness** (nothing later exists; the deadline has passed). A bilateral agreement about deeds barely needs it.
- **A fork convicts itself.** Two signed entries pointing at the same predecessor prove the signer lied, with no judge. Lies stop being prevented and become provable afterwards. This works when the liar is known and has something posted to lose, and fails against a stranger who vanishes.
- **The witness becomes weaker, in a good way.** It no longer attests the world, only "I saw this entry at this point in my own sequence". A witness who lies about that must fork its own sequence, which also convicts itself. This moves most of the twelve out of the Truth residue, and such witnesses can be plural.
- **The closed-world rule becomes local.** Fix names the witnesses: "what none of them has seen does not count". Title, Transfer and Current return to Clean, but only among people who accept the same witnesses. A thing's ownership is exactly as global as its witness set.
- Cost: if the value under Control exists only as entries in a shared register, losing the register also loses the trustless hand.

## Third test: the record cannot hold value

Supposition: the record holds only signed statements about value held elsewhere. Measured against the first test, not stacked on the second.

Ten tasks change, all of them built on Control.

| Task | Was | Now | Why |
|------|-----|-----|-----|
| Hold | Clean | Hand | The record can say who should hold. Someone elsewhere must actually hold and obey. |
| Recover | Clean | Hand | No spare key exists inside the record. The holder's own procedure decides. |
| Separate | Clean | Witness | "The accounts are separate" is now a claim about the world. |
| Divide | Clean | Part | The rule and the calculation stay clean. The paying out is a deed. |
| Secure | Clean | Part | A guarantor's signature survives. A pledge needs a holder. |
| Title | Clean | Part | The record says who holds. Whether they really do is a separate fact. |
| Transfer | Clean | Part | The entry is clean. The handing over is not. |
| Sum | Clean | Part | The claims add up on paper. Whether the whole exists behind them is a Measure. |
| End | Clean | Part | It ends in a Divide. |
| Leave | Clean | Part | Both routes out now pass through a holder. |

Clean falls from 33 to 23.

Two keep their verdict but lose their easy case: **Perform** is now always a deed, never a release. **Enforce** is now always force, since nothing can be posted in advance inside the record.

**Findings**

- **The record falls back from control to proof.** Every entry about value is either a claim ("she holds 100") or an instruction ("pay him 40"). Neither is the value.
- **A permanent gap opens between record and reality.** "The record says 100, there are 60" was a one-off Measure. Now it must be checked continuously.
- **Hold collapses into acting for another.** The holder is an agent: authorised, within limits, until told to stop. No new tasks are needed; Authorise, Scope, Separate, Show and Record are pointed at the holder. A two-key lock becomes a rule the holder must respect: pay only on both signatures.
- **Apply and Acknowledge carry more weight.** Apply still says exactly who is owed what, so disputes narrow to "did the holder pay?". The receiver's signed receipt becomes the only clean proof that value moved.

**What replaces the posted stake**

- Keep what is held small and brief: pay piece by piece, at the speed of a sale instead of a promise.
- Make the holder post something of its own, elsewhere.
- Use several holders.
- Rely on an enforcer who treats the record as the title, as a state does with a land register.

## Both suppositions together

Title, Transfer and Sum lose on both counts. What stays clean is bilateral: signed words, receipts and calculation. The record becomes an evidence and accounting layer: it proves who owes what and who lied, and it moves nothing. Its only force is what a proven liar stands to lose.

## Still open

- How much must a proven liar stand to lose, and where is it kept, for an evidence-only record to be enough?
- How many witnesses must two strangers share before a Transfer between them is safe?
- Is Identify truly a Witness task, or a sixth operation? Everything else rests on it, and its regress ends in direct acquaintance, which the grammar cannot express.
- Can a deed ever be made clean, or is the receiver's Acknowledge the best available?
- Does the grammar need a "who may do which operation on what" layer of its own, and is that layer just Authorise and Scope applied to itself?
