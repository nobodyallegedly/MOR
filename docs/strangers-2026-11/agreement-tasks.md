# The tasks of an agreement

Working list, started 10 Nov 2026. Version 2 (same day): extended through ten situations.

Each task answers one question. How it is carried out varies by case; the examples are illustrations, not requirements. The list is reasoned from first principles, not taken from an existing taxonomy, though it runs parallel to the old divisions of law (sale, obligation, co-ownership, association, agency, succession, procedure).

## Starting points

- A record is not for memory. It is a defense against the other party's future self, and one's own. Honest drift and lying look the same from outside.
- Each party must hold the other's commitment, not their own.
- A mark only deters a lie if an outsider could be shown it and be convinced. So every written agreement is written for a third person, even one who never turns up.
- Therefore: the least two people need to write down is exactly the least a third person needs to see.
- A record deters denial but does not stop a hand. Once value is involved, the system must move from proof to control.

## Method

For each new situation, ask: what new lie or failure becomes possible that the existing tasks do not defend against? Each new lie adds tasks. If there is none, the situation is not a new family.

Key: **A** = the parties can do it alone. **O** = needs something outside them. **A/O** = depends on the case.

## How the ten situations fed the list

| Situation | New lie or failure | Tasks added |
|-----------|--------------------|-------------|
| 1. Selling | "It is mine to sell." "It is what I said." "I never got it." | Title, Transfer, Inspect, Perform, Acknowledge |
| 2. Promising | "I can't." "I won't." "It isn't due yet." | Due, Secure, Enforce |
| 3. Owning together | "I hold a part." More parts than the whole. "The newcomer isn't bound." | Sum, Admit, Bind, Leave |
| 4. Acting together | "The group decided." "You were told." "That money was mine." | Decide, Notify, Count, Separate, Show |
| 5. Acting for another | "I act for her." "It is within my powers." "I am still authorised." "That deal was mine." | Authorise, Scope, Name, Current |
| 6. Someone is gone | "He is dead." "He is alive." "I am his heir." What he controlled is stuck. | Alive, Wait, Succeed, Recover, Able |
| 7. Disagreeing | "I never knew you objected." "The judge was yours." "That wasn't the last word." | Raise, Hear, Judge, Final (Settle split) |
| 8. Changing your mind | Not a new family. Uses Order, Decide, Notify, Current, Record, Measure, Divide. | Close split into Complete, Amend, End |
| 9. Agreeing with anyone | Not a new family. One-sided Commit + Notify + Current + Perform + Order + Sum + Secure. | None; rewords Identify, Commit, Exchange |
| 10. Keeping it private | "This is all of it." | Conceal, Reveal, Whole |

## What collapsed

- **Selling and promising** are one thing at two speeds. A sale is a promise with no delay; a promise is a sale stretched in time.
- **The original part B** (dividing future value) is a promise plus owning together.
- **Title and Transfer** are the reading and the writing of the same register. Succeed is a Transfer without the giver's help.
- **A group deciding and a person acting for another** are the same: a few bind others, within limits. Amending an agreement is one more decision of that kind.
- **Notify** is one task under five names: summons, revocation, complaint, amendment notice, public offer.
- **Current, Whole and Alive** are one problem: proving that nothing else exists (no later version, no side piece, no death). A document cannot prove an absence; only a single place to look can.
- **Situations 6, 7, 8 and 10 are not kinds of agreement.** They are conditions that can strike any agreement. Only 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 9 are kinds.

## What was reworded or split from version 1

- Identify: a party may be defined by an act ("whoever does X"), not only by name.
- Commit: assent may be by act, not only by mark.
- Exchange: may run one way until the other side acts.
- Hold: any owed value awaiting a condition, not only value awaiting division.
- Divide: burdens and costs as well as gains.
- Measure: split into Measure (how much) and Inspect (is it what was said).
- Record: any party's doings over time, not only contributions. No longer conditional.
- Settle: split into Raise, Hear, Judge, Final, Enforce, and moved to every agreement.
- Close: split into Complete, Amend, End, Leave.
- Fix: in open offers the whole burden of Align falls on the one who writes the words.

## Open questions, second answers

**Can Align and Free be checked by an outsider, or only made more likely?**

Only made more likely, and Able joins them. They are inner states at a past moment. But two things changed. Judge performs Align late, by deciding what the words meant. And the open offer shows the burden can be placed: whoever wrote the words carries their ambiguity. So the tasks cannot be checked, but their failure can be assigned in advance.

A second unprovable class appeared: absences (Current, Whole, Alive). These are not inner states, and they can be solved, but only by a single place to look.

**What is the smallest outside anchor?**

The outside tasks sort into four roles:

- **A register**: one ordered place to look. Serves Identify, Order, Title, Sum, Transfer, Current, Whole, proof of Notify.
- **A witness of the world**: says what happened outside the record. Serves Measure, Inspect, Due, Alive, Count.
- **A hand**: has power over the value. Serves Hold, Divide, Enforce, Recover.
- **A judge**: decides when the others are disputed.

The hand shrinks furthest: if value is posted in advance, Enforce becomes Divide. The register can be shared so that no single keeper has to be trusted. The judge is a fallback. The witness of the world cannot be removed: someone must say what happened.

So the smallest anchor is a shared register, a pre-posted Hold, and a witness. The witness is the hard residue.

## Still open

- Are 49 tasks the primitives, or are they fewer functions pointed at different objects (as Notify, Title/Transfer and Current/Whole/Alive suggest)?
