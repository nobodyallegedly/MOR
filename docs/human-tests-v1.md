# The author's human tests, version 1

*16 October 2026. For Nobody, allegedly, before the freeze-ready drafts (roadmap, 15 October: "the author's human tests first"). Written by the project lead. Test identities only; regtest or signet only; no real money.*

**What these tests are for.** The machines have checked that the rules hold. These tests check something they cannot: whether MOR makes sense to a person using it. Wherever something confuses you, surprises you, or feels wrong, that is a finding, even if nothing is broken. Write one line each time: what you did, what you expected, what happened. Nothing needs to pass; everything you notice counts.

**Before you start.** The homes, the gateway and your Mac are brought to the release of 15 October by a Mac session (the prompt of 16 October, in the lead window). Run these after its report says every home and the gateway are on that release.

## The tests

1. **Read dubsar.org on your phone.** Does it say the site is verified? Do the films, the feed and the report page open? Does anything read as unclear to someone who has never heard of MOR?
2. **Post from Dub on the Mac.** A short text, then one with a picture. Then find both on dubsar.org's reader. How long did it take to appear? Is it shown verified, and signed by the identity you used?
3. **Post from Dub on the web** (dub.dubsar.org), with a different test identity. Is the "for testing only" warning clear? Does the post reach the same reader?
4. **Reply to your own post** from the other identity. Does the reply show under the post? Does the first identity receive it in its inbox?
5. **Send a private message** between your two test identities. Does it arrive? Can anyone but the recipient read it? (Try opening it with the wrong identity.)
6. **Write to Machine, allegedly, from the feed page**, once public and once private. Then at your desk (MOR Identities): do you see the note? Does Machine, allegedly's answer reach you, and does a public answer appear on the feed?
7. **Nudge Machine, allegedly, then pick at the desk.** Is it clear what you are approving? Can you batch several picks?
8. **Report a "bug" from your own identity** to the reporting identity (test identity), as the report page explains. Do you find the instructions clear without help?
9. **Rotate a test identity** (as if a phone were lost), with the test signer and its seed words. Afterwards: do your earlier posts still read as verified on dubsar.org? (At the last rehearsal they read "void"; this is the fix.)
10. **A small collective**, with the collective client and three test identities: found it, have one member leave, look at what the remaining members and the departed one each see. Is it clear who owns what, and who decides?
11. **A payment on regtest** (the Lightning harness on your Mac): pay one test identity from another. Is it clear what was paid, to whom, and what proves it?
12. **Anything else you are curious about.** Free play is a test too.

## How to report

One line per observation, in any order, in a note you keep on your Mac or paste to the lead window: *what I did / what I expected / what happened / how it felt*. The lead window turns them into findings and, where they touch the texts, into questions, one at a time, before the freeze-ready drafts.
