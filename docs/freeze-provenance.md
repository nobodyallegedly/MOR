# Freeze-ready texts: provenance map

*Each rule or section of the freeze-ready texts, with the findings that shaped it. The freeze-ready texts carry no finding numbers (decided by Nobody, allegedly, F360); this map stays in the repository, outside what the C verifier session receives, and is how to trace why a rule exists. Built from the finding numbers each rule or section cited in the draft just before its freeze-ready one (rule numbers are the same in both), then completed by hand under "Added in the freeze-ready drafts" where the freeze-ready draft wrote in a decision. Findings are in `docs/findings/`. Review labels (Fable's I5, Q7 and the like) are not listed: they are cited in the same older drafts.*
