## His proposal: a conversations cMIP (H86)

His words: "Makes sense no cMIP is being used to treat one as a CC, maybe we should. What does the CC cMIP offers? It keep tracks of who the primary receiver was and informs the client. We could even add 'group chat' that tags both as primary and flags for multiple primaries and the client can handle those differently. When CC is used, a reply to all option appears, with option to remove some, when group is used it replies to all by default, offers whisper to a single identity as an option, and allows to leave the chat. Communicating with the people that will be tempted to build on MOR is key and this would really improve it."

*The suite lead's reading, to be discussed:* a Dubsar cMIP for private conversations, above the core: who is primary and who is Cc; a conversation's own identity (so two conversations with the same people stay apart); a group, every member primary; reply to all (with names removable); leave. **Whisper is the app's, not the cMIP's** (his correction: "I think the whisper to one is more of an app thing. When I chat, it simply offers a pop up to write to a single id"): from a group, the app offers a pop-up to write to one member, an ordinary private message. **Checked with the MOR lead, 24 October: entirely above the core**, on four conditions the core already sets: (1) primary and Cc carried inside the message's own structure, never by the order of `to` (the core gives that order no meaning); (2) every recipient stays in the inner act's `to` (Envelopes rule 11; the sealed container's step 5): Cc can be marked, never hidden, no Bcc; a whisper is a separate private message, the app's; (3) a conversation named by `refs` (its first message), never by `objects` (a text act carrying `objects` is invalid, Text rule 14); (4) a client without the cMIP still reads the text and its recipients. Groups, reply to all, leaving: structured text acts. **Timing, decided by Nobody, allegedly: after the freeze, high priority, first in line** ("it smoothes conversations with the first few people that could come to build").
