*Nobody, allegedly. 27 November 2026. A review only: no file of DubPay's was changed, nothing is rewritten or decided. Every verdict below is the reviewer's, marked so. The stint's wish (Nobody, allegedly, 26 November 2026): "We need to change the current wish to updating the core after the findings". His words on this work (27 November): "I saw some dubs stating they were planning to redraft their own constitution. This break is a good moment to do so. Oversee the process please".*

*What was read: `_team/dubpay/constitution/draft-3.md` and `changes.md`; the lead's order (`_team/lead/constitutions/order.md`); the MOR lead's may-move list of 26 November; catalogue v2 and `answers-to-w3c.md` (W3-B2); W3-C; the Damgar fix README (`wave-3/fix/README.md`); the 16 acts the draft cites, decoded with the kit's own `describe` (the core library's reading of the bytes); the P4 check on branch `claude/dubpay-check-p4`; `docs/decisions.md` in the repository, for the quoted wishes; and two public Cashu texts the draft says it read. Nothing else of DubPay's. The Mac was not reachable; DubPay's bundle stood in for it (see Limits).*

*Marks: **counted** (a script the reviewer ran), **traced** (an act's or a text's words show it), **inferred** (the reviewer's reading). "Line N" is a line of `draft-3.md`; "changes N" a line of `changes.md`. Act ids are cut to 8 characters.*

## In plain words

1. **The shape holds.** Part two is 1,188 words (counted). All 14 principles name what they rest on. Part one could not be compared with the lead's project copy, but the five wishes it quotes match `docs/decisions.md` word for word, except one line that the decisions file does not hold.
2. **Most citations check out, three do not.** Of the 16 acts, 13 say what the draft says they say. Three are misdirected: principle 5's acts do not hold the lurker's finding, principle 11's "closing act" does not say what the draft puts in its mouth, and principle 8's acts show the complaint, not the fix.
3. **The best example in the draft contradicts its own rule.** Principle 12 says a correction is a new text naming the one it replaces. Its example, the straight split published twice, is a second act whose only change is one character in the title, and which does not name the act it corrects (counted).
4. **One count is turned around.** Principle 14 says "42 Terms asked of the kit, 0 naming a money cMIP". Catalogue v2 counts 77 Terms naming the straight split and 8 naming Hop's split. `changes.md` has the count right ("0 money rules checked by the kit"); the draft changed its meaning.
5. **Two things decided that are not DubPay's to decide, and two answers to P4 that are not in the draft.** Principle 1 settles "DubPay holds no one's money" while `changes.md` says the question is open for Nobody, allegedly. Principle 3's provisional mark names no rule of the may-move list. `changes.md` says principle 7 states that a timeout is a failure and principle 12 states the gate's limit; neither does.
