## In plain words

The round ran on Agreements written from scratch, draft 25, whose acts are only two: **Terms** (what is agreed) and a **Statement** (an assent, a counter signature, a contest, a resolve, or a kind a cMIP defines), with the **report** of the shared language beside them. Edubba (`clients/desk`) and the collective client (`clients/collective`, now the library Edubba's Collectives tab runs on) were built on the *earlier* Agreements: the MIR series, drafts 7 to 19 by their own citations, running on main's core library, which implements that series up to its draft 24. That earlier Agreements has some twenty act types (claims, publications, clones with marks, records, resignations, forks, closings, splits, grants and more) under another spec hash. Draft 25 has none of them. So, for draft 25, the two clients write nothing it reads as its own ("not this encoding", or "rejected"), and read nothing it writes: an act of draft 25 reaching Edubba was filed as "other", unread.

Some of this is a fault with one right answer, and is fixed on the branch (marked **fixed**). Some cannot be fixed without choosing what draft 25 leaves to cMIPs: which cMIP reads an ownership agreement's words, how a collective is written, which control cMIP a split service runs. Those are left as they are and marked **question** (Q1 to Q9, at the end, for the MOR lead).

## Which draft each client implements

**Edubba (`clients/desk`).** It says Agreements draft 17 of the MIR series: `clients/desk/README.md:92` ("Precisely (Envelopes draft 11, Money draft 11, Agreements draft 17)"), `clients/desk/src/publishing.ts:23` (the same words), `clients/desk/src/deals.ts:22` ("Agreements draft 17, rule 16"); with later touches from MIR drafts 18 and 19 (`clients/desk/src/kinds.ts:55`, `clients/desk/src/mirs.ts:13`: the default control cMIP's acts). That text is `spec/archive/MIR-agreements-draft-17.md`, not draft 17 of the from-scratch series. Its acts are built by the shared libraries `clients/repo`, `clients/grants` and `clients/collective`, and judged by main's core library (`clients/genesis/src/core.ts`), which implements the MIR series (its current text `spec/MIR-agreements-draft-24.md`).

**The collective client (`clients/collective`).** It says MIR drafts 7, 8 and 10 in its README (`clients/collective/README.md:57`, `:59` "## Agreements draft 8", `:63` "## Agreements draft 10"), and cites up to draft 18 in its code (`clients/collective/src/actions.ts:2174`, F421; `clients/collective/src/explain.ts:89`, draft 16). It writes through `clients/repo/src/agreements.ts` ("Agreements acts as the test collective makes them (Agreements draft 8)", line 1) and the same core library.

Neither had any code for the from-scratch series: no spec hash of draft 25, no Terms or Statement of encoding 2, no report (searched: `SPEC_DRAFT_25`, `dd90f8af`, `agreementsNew` appear nowhere in either client, nor in main's core).

## For the MOR lead

Each is a place where the fix needs a choice that draft 25 leaves to cMIPs or to another layer, so this session did not choose. Each names the rule and the code that meet.

- **Q1. Collectives.** The collective client is the MIR series' collective throughout: founding terms with a constitution, clones with marks, areas, records, resignations, declarations of absence, rollbacks, forks, closings, a keyless identity acting through its members (`clients/collective/src/actions.ts`; `clients/repo/src/agreements.ts:206-256`, `:509-760`). Draft 25 has none of it: a collective is an agreement that may be a party (G1), signing for its parties under a power (G1.2), its changes governed by its update condition (K4) and in-force rule (K2), its acts' rules by powers (K3); who may leave, how members change, what a closing is, are cMIPs' (section 5.2: "A party wants to leave", "Any two of three owners"). Which cMIPs, and how a collective of the MIR series is carried over, is not in draft 25. Left as it is.
- **Q2. Edubba's deals: which body cMIP (K1), which update condition (K4).** The claim, the ownership agreement and the publication (`clients/desk/src/publishing.ts:823-1250`), a vow and its buyers (`:1758-1830`), each need Terms whose words some cMIP reads (K1) and whose updates some cMIP governs (K4), a role's spot (K5) for a buyer. Draft 25 names none; the round's players used cMIPs of their own (DubLaw's "shower work", among others, by the wave-2 catalogue). Left as it is.
- **Q3. Which fingerprint the body's words hold.** Encoding 2 writes MOR's work hash and marks it [lead] (E5); the kit's fields guide says draft 25 leaves it open (section 9, item 5) and offers both. The library writes the fingerprint it is given and computes neither.
- **Q4. A split service: receiver, control (K6), reports.** The publication's split service and its split acts (F16, F18) become a receiver and a control cMIP with calls and leg reports (G11). Which control cMIP, and what its leg reports state beyond the block, is a cMIP's (section 5.1, first row). Left as it is.
- **Q5. The seller's alarm.** Which version a payment names is Money's to say (section 8.1); `sellerAlarm` (`clients/desk/src/desk.ts:928-940`) cannot be brought to draft 25 until it does.
- **Q6. Claims and the baker's rights.** A claim (type 3) has no draft-25 act; the baker's rights are read from the envelope's chain (G14), which no layer gives yet (section 8.1; encoding 2, section 10, items 13 and 18).
- **Q7. Grants and revocations.** A grant of acts to someone acting for a collective (`clients/desk/src/grants.ts:123`, `:173`) becomes a power with a rule (K3) or a delegation (G5.5); which cMIP is not in draft 25.
- **Q8. The web client.** The draft-25 binding (6.6 MB) is not in Edubba's web release: on the web an act of draft 25 is said, not read. Whether the web release should carry it is a release decision.
- **Q9. Main's core.** Draft 25 lives in the core library on `claude/agreements-verifier-2`, not on main. The two clients load its binding beside main's (`clients/collective/built-25/`). Once main's core has it, that copy should go.
