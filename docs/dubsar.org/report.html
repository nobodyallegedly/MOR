Report a bug or an attack

Bugs and attacks are reported on MOR by people on MOR, each from their own identity. A report is an act its reporter signs, sent to a public reporting identity, kept and answered in the open.

How to report, from your own identity

  • In Dub, on your computer, or Dub on the web, https://dub.dubsar.org/, for testing only: write the report, put the reporting identity’s ID above in “To an identity”, and post. An identity of your own is made there in a minute, at a home such as https://home1.dubsar.org.
  • From any other client: a public message to the reporting identity: a text act, signed by your identity, naming the reporting identity as its recipient and left, unsealed, in its inbox.

A report is public: anyone may read it, it is copied to GitHub as an issue with its words exactly as you signed them, and it is answered in the open, from the reporting identity, to your identity. MOR 1 is an experiment holding nothing of value, so nothing is kept back. A sealed message to the reporting identity is read by it alone and never copied anywhere, for the rare report that must not be seen yet.

What a good report contains

  • What you did, step by step, so that someone else can do it again.
  • What you expected, and what happened instead, in the words shown on the screen if there were any.
  • Where: the page’s address, or the client and its version, and your browser or computer.
  • The acts involved, by their IDs (64 characters), if there are any.
  • For an attack: what it lets someone do, to whom, and what they need to do it.
  • Never a key, nor the words of a chain key or a carry: no one needs them to look into a report.

Not on MOR yet?

GitHub issues are open as a mirror: https://github.com/nobodyallegedly/MOR/issues. It runs both ways. Every public report made on MOR is copied there, and every answer to it. An issue opened there is published again on MOR by the reporting identity, labelled “Reported on GitHub by” its author’s GitHub name, once the one holding its key has read it, and answered on MOR; each answer is copied back to the issue.
