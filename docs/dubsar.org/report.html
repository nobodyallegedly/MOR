Public or private

Public is for almost every report: a report is meant to be seen. It is left unsealed in the reporting identity’s inbox, anyone can read it, it is copied to GitHub as an issue with its words exactly as you wrote them, and the answer is given in the open. The box starts on Private: press Public for a report anyone may see.

Private is for an attack that is not yet safe to show: one that could hurt someone if others learned of it before it is fixed. It is sealed in your browser so that only the reporting identity can open it. It is never shown here nor copied to GitHub.

What a good report contains

  • What you did, step by step, so that someone else can do it again.
  • What you expected, and what happened instead, in the words shown on the screen if there were any.
  • Where: the page’s address, or the client and its version, and your browser or computer.
  • The acts involved, by their IDs (64 characters), if there are any.
  • For an attack: what it lets someone do, to whom, and what they need to do it.
  • How to reach you, if you want more than the public answer. Your report is signed by an identity made for it alone, which no answer can reach.
  • Never a key, nor the words of a chain key or a carry: no one needs them to look into a report.

Not on MOR yet?
