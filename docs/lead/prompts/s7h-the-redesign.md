**1. Six layers** (F402). The MICS's layer table becomes one row per MIR (Identity, Text, Envelopes, Money, Agreements, Development); the old pillar names go, or stay only as a description. Agreements' `kind` numbers the layers in that order, **0 Identity, 1 Text, 2 Envelopes, 3 Money, 4 Agreements, 5 Development**. Every place reading "Envelopes and Text" as one layer is split, each act type going to the MIR that defines it (areas, extensions' declared layers, a release reached by the layers it touches, the core's `layers::`). **2. The four acts.** Follow the design note, section by section.
- **The bake** (F398): Envelopes' type 0, renamed, losing its price (field 7) and its `for` (field 8); "upload" as the verb for putting any act on homes and relays; Envelopes' withdrawal (rule 24) retires. A bake is never withdrawn by the protocol.
- **The claim:** its own act, in Agreements, never a mark on a bake.
- **The ownership agreement:** ownership only (shares, how owners decide: change rules, keepers, judges, succession, absence). One act type, two targets, written `[kind, hash]` (0 a claim, 1 an identity it founds); a work's refuses areas, powers, key grammar and founding fields, a collective's requires them; the stake is on the target ("object null" retires). The split plan, split service, fees and role shares leave it (terms fields 8, 14, 28, 29 retired, never reused).
- **The publication, which is the offer:** the standing offer (type 6) and rule 15's "publication's agreement" become this one act: what is sold, its price tag, its terms, its partners (any identity; each a map with numbered keys: identity, share, optional label in canonical text that nothing reads, with an italic note why it is there), the split service that pays everyone, and the full split (the split plan's shape, as a map), the owners' line read by reference to the ownership agreement, named by its founding version, reading its line. Ended by a later version that sells nothing; a payment racing the ending owed back by the split service from what it holds, then by each party in the share it received (rule 30). A collective's own split service is a split agreement of the same kind pointing at its ownership.
- **Shares by reference** (F402, 3): the split service's receipt names the ownership agreement's version on its line; the version in force for that receipt is the latest complete version on that line its history holds; a receipt naming a version on no line of the named agreement, or a fork's branch other than its reference, is a wrong receipt.
- Stated cost, in Money's text and Nobody, allegedly's words: a re-uploader receives tips and tag payments on their own bake; "piracy cannot be stopped. But truly rewarding creators can be made easy."

**3. The price tag** (F398, F402, 4). One new Money act type, the next free number: signed by the seller, naming the bake (or, under a publication, the publication, an optional field), the price (Money's amount), the payee. On a bare bake it counts only when signed by the bake's uploader. Under a publication it is signed by the publication's split service or a party it designates, and names the publication; any other is nobody's offer under it. Versioned: the latest counts; a version with no price withdraws it. A payment naming a publication's tag is a purchase under the publication (rule 12), so a Money-only wallet can make a valid purchase. Say in one place that the publication's ending (every party) and the tag's withdrawal (the publication's side alone) are different people's stops. Every rule that read a publication's price or `for` reads the tag.

**4. Collectives** (F401, F402). No signing key, no member keys; F353's mechanics retire.
- The genesis declares its founding agreement in place of a first signing key and is signed by the chain key it commits to; a chain key signs rotations, chain signatures and such a genesis, nothing else (Identity rules 1 and 7). A one-way format door, written knowing it.
- A member acts for the collective with their own key, the act naming the collective in `objects` (rule 58), judged by Agreements; one that does not name it is the member's own. A member's power ends at the line that registers their departure.
- **The founding names who acts for the collective in each of five layers** (Text, Envelopes, Money, Agreements, Development): terms field 19 as a map keyed by layer number, each a rule in the terms' existing grammar (`[1, 1]` any one member alone); finer areas by act type optional, narrowing within a layer; a founding missing a layer is outside the format and founds nothing (F295); a key for a layer the MIRs do not define is refused. Rule 60's sentence is Fable's (`fable-f402-layers.md`, section 5), as written. **Client conformance:** before a party signs a founding, its client shows, layer by layer, what the rule lets one member do alone, Money first.
- Decisions chain explicitly: each decision names the previous in `objects` (rule 58); any member draws a record; the collective's own agreement (founding and clones) keeps the constitutional rule (rule 64), forks and closings theirs (rule 91).
- Fable's other sentences (fourth review, sections 1 and 2): a single chain-key holder still needs a named successor and an escrowed share; a collective's escape endorsement by members meeting its rule; a collective sealed to by sealing to each member; Money rule 7's collective passage one line; Text rule 14's first case goes; a publication over a closed collective or closed ownership sells nothing from the closing's line; payouts become a stake's buyer's once the transfer is complete. The stated cost that a client knowing only Identity or only Money judges nothing a collective does goes into the MICS where the layers are described.
