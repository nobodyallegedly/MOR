## Questions

1. **Does the pinning close the late withdrawal, or move it?** Attack decision 1: who can refuse to pin, and what do they gain by leaving Terms "not yet in force" for ever while others act as if they were? Can a pin itself be raced, or made late? What does a payer see between completion and pinning? Does pinning apply to an update too, and if so, can one signer who will not pin hold every change hostage?
2. **The two acts of leaving.** Between the dissolution and the new agreement, what holds: money still arriving, powers given, spots filled, a contest under way? Can those who stay refuse to sign the new agreement and so use a leaving to end things for everyone? Can the leaver be made to sign a dissolution she did not want (an expulsion in two acts)? Does the new agreement inherit anything, and by whose signature?
3. **A sale, again.** With "The holder at the time" restored: who can backdate Terms against a buyer of the rights, and what must the buyer do at the moment of buying to be safe? Is that readable by someone who does not negotiate?
4. **Nothing about me without my signature.** Does it break anything that should work: a collective naming an officer, a guardian, a successor, an estate? Does "a power given binds its giver" still let a power be given that nothing ends?
