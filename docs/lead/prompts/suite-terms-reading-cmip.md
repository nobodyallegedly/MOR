**Read first:** on `claude/lead-records-2`, F441 in `docs/findings/MOR-findings-log-round-2.md`, gate decision 1; `docs/reviews/fable-signed-facts.md` (pull request 135), sections 1, 2, 5 and 7 above all; `docs/reviews/fable-fresh-eyes.md` (pull request 134), section 5; the stranger's texts in `docs/strangers-2026-11/` on the same branch; Agreements 24's client-conformance passages that begin "before signing".

**What was decided** (Nobody, allegedly): the "show before signing" family leaves the core, replaced by one client sentence (*before a signature that binds a person to terms, the client shows what its terms-reading cMIP produces from them, names that cMIP, and shows what the person can always do*), and a new task, **terms reading**, chosen by the signer's client, never by the agreement: it accepts terms and the declarations of the cMIPs they name, and produces facts, never forecasts or judgments.

**Draft 1 of `cmip-terms-reading`**, marked plainly as written before the core's declarations exist:
1. **The facts** it produces, from Fable's section 2 list (who the parties are and your place; your share and how it can be paid less; who changes the rules; who may declare you absent, on what silence, by whose authority, and "decides alone, on no proof" where that is true; who judges; what you owe; who ends it; where the money goes if a promise fails; which cMIPs decide anything), each as data, never prose, so any client renders it in any language.
2. **What you can always do,** whatever the terms (the way out the core keeps), shown with every reading.
3. **The order** in which a client shows them, plainest danger first, for a person who reads literally, does not negotiate and does not see the trap nobody names.
4. **What it never says:** forecasts, scores, fairness, the other party's reputation, comparisons.
5. **Where it waits on the core:** each fact that needs a declaration not yet written, named.
6. **Its own face**, as Creative Commons' deed says of itself: a summary, not the terms.
