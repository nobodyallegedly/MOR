**Where things stand.** After your third review (F397), Nobody, allegedly, hand-tested the apps and found the path from a file to a sale too tangled: one word ("publication") named two things, and splits, fees and partners sat inside the ownership agreement. He redesigned it in four acts: a **bake** ("I uploaded this"), a **claim** ("I claim to have made this"), an **ownership agreement** (ownership only), and a **publication**, which is the offer and carries all of distribution (price tag, partners, split service, the full split, reading the owners' shares by reference). A price tag becomes its own Money act. Separately, a collective loses its signing key and its member keys: it keeps only its chain key, held as shares, and grants powers to its members, who sign with their own keys (F401). `context/design-note-bake-to-publication.md` holds all of it. **Nothing is in the texts yet.** One session will write it into the texts, the core, the vectors and the binding, together with F397's sentences, routes and F400.
