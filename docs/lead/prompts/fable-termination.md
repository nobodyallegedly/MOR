The core knows only whether an agreement, and each party's place in it, is **active or terminated**; conditions come from a cMIP plugged into a new task, **termination terms** (it reads the agreement, the terminating act and the line; it answers met, not yet met or unknown). The core imposes no veto and keeps no guard against one: parties may sign any termination terms, a veto or a minimum term included. By default a publication's owners' side terminates, alone and at once, the publication or any other party's place in it (split service, backups, partners, the measurer), for the future only.
