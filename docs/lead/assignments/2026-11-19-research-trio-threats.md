**In my wording (the MOR lead's):** the trio is three signed statements about a debt, each counting by itself when the party who loses by it signs: **Owed** (signed by the debtor), **Ended** (by the creditor), **Passed** (by the creditor who leaves). A debt states its owner through its own chain of passes, as an envelope does. MOR shows forks and keeps the trail; settlement and its safety are outside, in the rail, the cMIP and the module.
