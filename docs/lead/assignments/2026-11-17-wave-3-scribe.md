## The sort: grammar or built on top

**In my wording (the MOR lead's), from decision 5:** a rule is **grammar** if it is part of the shared language every act is written in, or a guarantee the bytes can check that no module may be able to break (the admission test). A rule is **built on top** if it settles how people should behave in a deal (who must pay, pass on, report, stay answerable, may leave) rather than what can be written and checked; such a rule corners the protocol, however right its idea, and belongs to a cMIP in a socket.
