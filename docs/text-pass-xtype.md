# The xtype, the nudge's cause, the split's top and the referenced signer's chain

*13 October 2026. Branch `claude/mor-xtype-nudge-split-relay-u9rloo`, from main at `177229c`. One roadmap session: four decisions and readings written into new drafts and built. **1.** F278, the name "xtype" (Development draft 8, the MICS v25, the freeze suite v25; wording only). **2.** The nudge cMIP draft 3: it is an xtype, not an extension; and the answer to Machine, allegedly's Q2 ("it should not exist without a nudge"), built in `clients/nudge` and enforced at the desk. **3.** The split cMIP draft 2: F280 in the cMIP and its code. **4.** The relay transport cMIP draft 4: the delivery record's role share off the top (F280), and the F272 build's Question 2, a client carrying the referenced signer's identity chain, built in the genesis client and the desk. Earlier drafts stay as approved. Test identities only. rustc 1.97.0.*

## In plain words

**1. "Xtype".** A community specification (a cMIP) can plug into MOR in three ways. It can do one of the jobs the core hands out by number (a **task**); an agreement can add it to its own rules (an **extension**); or it can invent new kinds of acts out of the core's pieces, which nobody foresaw (until now called only "a new kind of thing"). You named the third: **xtype**, "extra type". The texts now read: a task, an extension, an xtype. Nothing changes in what any rule does; only the name is new, in Development (draft 8), the core's own document (version 25) and the freeze test suite (version 25).

**2. Machine, allegedly, cannot speak to strangers unprompted.** You decided that a reply to a stranger "should not exist without a nudge". The project lead read it with your pick as the other cause. So now:

- **Machine, allegedly's own program refuses to sign** a reply or a message to anyone but you unless it carries your nudge or your pick that allows it. This is enforced at the desk: whatever Claude prepares for Machine, allegedly, and whatever its answering does, is first read as it would be once signed; if it would read "outside its terms", the desk blocks it and signs nothing.
- **Anyone reading MOR reads everything Machine, allegedly writes against its published terms**, cause or none. A public post, a reply to you or a message to you need no cause and read "within its terms". A reply to a stranger naming neither your nudge nor your pick reads "outside the terms". Before, the nudge text said nothing at all about such an act, which contradicted one of its own scenarios.
- **The text now says it is an xtype**, correcting the slip that called it an extension.

**3. The split rulebook follows "shared ownership means shared fruits".** The split service's rulebook (the split cMIP) now does what the core already requires since F280: first the overhead, each non-owner's fraction of the payment, off the top; then everything left to the owners alone, to each work's stake by its weight in the plan, and inside each stake to its holders by what they own. A plan that puts a non-owner beside the owners in what is left is refused. A plan with no owners at all (a worker and a platform, F283) must give out the whole payment off the top, and the few units rounding leaves go where the largest fractions were cut.

**4. A pointer always leads somewhere.** When one act points at someone else's public act (a reply at a post), the pointer names the post's signer (F272). But a reader who knows only the relay holding the reply could not look that signer up there, because the signer's identity (its birth certificate and rotations) was kept elsewhere. Now a program writing such a pointer also copies the signer's identity, which is public, to the relays where it publishes. The desk does it for everything it publishes; the relay rulebook (relay transport, draft 4) says clients SHOULD. The same draft also says, for F280, that a relay's share for serving a file is overhead, taken off the top.

**No question is put.** What the decisions left open were mechanics of the development layer, each taken below as a reading, marked, to confirm.

## Readings taken (mechanics, under the delegation; to confirm)

1. **The nudge MUST, stated through the terms.** The decision's words name Machine, allegedly's case ("anyone but its owner"); the cMIP serves any agent, so draft 3 states it as: no text act the agent's latest terms do not let go out freely, without an allowed nudge or pick that allows it. Under Machine, allegedly's terms the two read the same; for an agent whose terms let replies to anyone go freely, its own declaration decides.
2. **Which terms an act naming no cause is read under** (item 8a): the latest version that does not lie after it in the agent's sequence, followed from the one first version held; unknown where that cannot be told; nothing said before the agent's first terms, where none are held, or for a private act addressed to no one.
3. **Under terms without field 5**, an uncaused act needing a pick reads "within, by pick, no pick shown", as draft 2's N3b already read the same reply under such terms, and as draft 1's cost says: without field 5 a pick is never signed, so naming none shows nothing missing.
