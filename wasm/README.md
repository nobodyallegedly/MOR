# mor-wasm

The core library for the TypeScript clients, through WebAssembly (roadmap step 5). Every act a client makes or judges goes through the core library's own code: CBOR, hashes, signatures, the identity checks, X-Wing and sealed containers. The clients only fetch, store and show.

Hashes cross the boundary as lowercase hex strings, larger data as `Uint8Array`. Unlike the core library, the bindings draw fresh randomness from the platform (`crypto.getRandomValues`), except where the caller passes it (sealing, so that the tests can re-make every key exchange with a second implementation).

Build (from `clients/genesis`): `npm run wasm`, which runs

```
cargo build -p mor-wasm --release --target wasm32-unknown-unknown --locked
wasm-bindgen --target web --out-dir clients/genesis/wasm target/wasm32-unknown-unknown/release/mor_wasm.wasm
```

with `wasm-bindgen-cli` of the version pinned in `Cargo.toml` (0.2.129), and with the build machine's folders replaced in the compiled file (`--remap-path-prefix`: this repository becomes `/mor`, Cargo's home `/cargo`), so that the build is reproducible: the same Rust and wasm-bindgen versions give the same bytes on any machine, in any folder (roadmap step 10a; tested in `clients/site/test/reproducible.test.ts`).

Verdicts an app may see change:
- **A payment naming a price tag's earlier, priced version, receipted on a push rail after the withdrawal is in the split service's history, is no purchase** (Agreements rule 12): it was a purchase. On a request rail, nothing changes.
- **A person's homeless rotation that a valid objection from a live home of the old set names is void** (Identity rule 49): it read "pending". A collective's is unchanged (its own escape endorsement).
- **A clone marked with a succession plan naming stake successors must pass the party's stakes to them**; one leaving them as they were, or changing a layer's rule beyond putting the seat successor in the party's place, is invalid (rules 64 and 83). One passing them, valid until now only without a stake change, is not invalid.
- **A collective's split agreement:** a later version paying the stake less needs its holders' consent by the stake's holders' rule (rules 15, 48, 94 and 95): every holder who is no member signs under "every holder"; under a rule by share the holders signing meet the share, a proxy voting for a non-member where it has not signed itself; the roster read at the version, the transfers its history places applied. A version that once counted on the parties' signatures alone may now be a draft. A fork's successor whose first split agreement pays its kept holders less than the original's did needs them too (rule 95).
- The anchoring binding (`anchoringOffer`, `anchoringReadOffer`, `anchoringCheckTicket`) is unchanged: the moved anchoring cMIP is not yet on this branch, and the binding follows it once it is.

Verdicts an app may see change:
- **Unknown spreads with Fable's bound** (Money rule 8; decided by Nobody, allegedly, F413): where an answer depends on two or more receipts or claims whose rail answer is pending or not held, it is unknown until they are verified. **A vow (`agreementsVow`) with two or more such records among its sales now throws unknown as a whole**, where each sale was marked unknown one by one; with one such record, as before. The same holds for `agreementsPurchase` (a payment whose receipt and claim both lack an answer: unknown, as before in practice), `agreementsPurchaseContests` and `agreementsOwedBack` (the list unknown). An app shows "waiting for the rail" for the whole list until the rail answers.
- **A purchase paid through a route is bought by the route's first payer** where their claim of the first hop names, in key 4, the tag the purchase names: `agreementsPurchaseContests` on that claim shows the first payer's contest; on the conversion service's own claim of the last hop, nothing (the service carries). A first payer's claim naming another tag, or none, leaves the purchase the service's own. **Client conformance: an app paying through a route signs the first hop's claim naming, in key 4, the tag the route pays for**, so that its user has standing. *The suite should read the open question on this in `docs/s7j-report.md` (question 1) before building it: on a request rail the claim's key 4 must match the commitment.*
- **A price tag's price changed on a push rail** (Agreements rule 12): a payment naming the earlier version is no purchase, owed back, where the split service's receipt holds the later version in its history; as a withdrawal already was.
- **A specification published for a collective by the member its founding names for Envelopes, or under a grant key Agreements backs, is the creator's** (Development rule 3): read with Agreements. No binding exposes Development rule 3 today; the core's `AgreementsView::specification_claim` answers it for Ummia when the suite adds it.
- **A collective's closing (`agreementsClosing`) is not complete while it owes a refund on a payment to it** that is not ended and not repaid (money received for nothing); unknown while whether such a payment is a purchase rests on a rail's answer. **A lapsed notice leaves money owed back open only where the payer gave no address read from their chain** (no refund rail on their claim, no vault, no payee pointer held); unknown where the payer's chain cannot be resolved.
- **`[5, stake]` under an every-holder rule** (Agreements rule 64.4): where a stake's declared holders' rule is every holder, a clone that is an owners' matter of it names `[5, stake]` with every holder in its mark (`agreementsClonePlan` now lists it among the powers needed); a mark without it, or leaving a holder out, is invalid. The core refused `[5, stake]` under every holder.
- **An inner deal consents by its version in force** (Agreements rule 15): a consent its parties gave under an earlier version stands only where the version in force, or its parties' signature acts on it, hold it in their history; signatures given under a version no longer in force give none.
- **Identity** (rules 34 and 45; "What stops a chain"; the table of the homeless path's acts): an act a pending acknowledgement would rescue is pending, not void; a position whose evidence the verifier cannot judge, and which would change which rotation counts, stops at "cannot tell" (`resolve`'s `next`), and at "pending" where that evidence is pending; an objection, absence statement or escape endorsement naming more than the one entry is invalid.
- **A self-hosted identity's escape reaches its readers**: the relay at a self-hosted home now keeps a homeless rotation as evidence (it refused it as a conflict), so a reader meets it and asks the new home for its receipt (Kišib's F299 test).

Verdicts an app may see change:
- **A claim a member signs for a collective, naming the collective in `objects`, is the collective's claim** (Agreements, "Claim (type 3)"; rule 58; F412): the collective is its opener, a creator, and the work's default holder where no ownership agreement targets it; the claim counts where the member may act for the collective in Agreements (rule 60). Such a claim was refused (its signer had to be among the creators). Members may be listed beside the collective as co-creators, each binding it by a signature act of their own, the member who signed it for the collective included. A claim naming the collective that lists only its signing member is now the collective's act and counts for nothing (its opener, the collective, is not among its creators); a member claiming for themselves names no collective. No binding changed: a member makes it with `makeEveryday` as any act for the collective, naming the collective in `objects`.
- **A receipt or claim naming a previous hop's receipt (receipt field 6, claim key 10) counts toward an obligation only where the route ending at it is valid** (Money rules 15 and 24): every record on it valid on its rail and held, each hop paid to the previous forward's next payee its exact amount. It counted on its own until now (`agreementsPaid`).
- **A payout's receipt names its split in field 5 alone** (Agreements rule 23): one naming the split only in its `objects` no longer discharges the payout (`agreementsServiceAccount`, `agreementsSplit`). The suite's apps already write field 5 (round 9).
- **A self-hosted identity's homeless rotation is pending without an escape endorsement** (Identity rule 4): it counted on the identity's own signature.
- **A rotation built on a homeless rotation a valid objection voids is void**, as the homeless one is (it read invalid); acts bound to it stay invalid.
- **A private encryption key (Envelopes type 4) is invalid** (Envelopes rule 8): it was valid, so a client could hold a different current key from another's.
- **A deal's version is kept complete only by a party's or keeper's act** (Agreements rule 47): a later version cloning it counts only once every party signed it; a split service's receipt naming it in field 9 counts; a payer's claim naming it no longer does. A version a signer's rotation disowned, nothing else relying on it, falls back to its parent.
- **A living party triggers its own succession plan by signing the clone marked with it** (Agreements rule 82): such a clone was a draft until the authority's declaration.
